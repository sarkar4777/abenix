HVDC INTERCONNECTOR CAPACITY AGREEMENT
(IFA2 — Interconnexion France-Angleterre 2)

BETWEEN
(1) INTERCONNECTOR INVESTMENTS (UK) LIMITED — "IFA2 OpCo"
    an English company holding the OFGEM cap-and-floor interconnector licence for the IFA2 HVDC link (Chilling, UK ↔ Tourbe, France, 1,000 MW nameplate, 204 km submarine HVDC, ±320 kV VSC).
(2) EUROCURRENT TRADING B.V. — "Capacity Holder"
    a Netherlands trading company and member of EPEX SPOT and APX UK, holding ENTSO-E balancing responsibility.

EXECUTION DATE: 5 February 2025
SERVICE COMMENCEMENT DATE: 1 March 2025
TERM: Five (5) years with two 2-year extension options
CONTRACT REFERENCE: IFA2-LTCH-2025-0217

RECITALS
(A) IFA2 is a regulated interconnector operating under the OFGEM cap-and-floor regime since 2021.
(B) The Capacity Holder has won Long-Term (LT) annual capacity rights in the March 2025 JAO auction: 80 MW firm day-ahead capacity in the FR→UK direction.
(C) The Parties have agreed the commercial, operational, scheduling, and financial terms set out below.

ARTICLE 1  DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Annual Firm Capacity" — 80 MW firm, direction FR→UK, day-ahead auction product.
1.2 "Nomination" — hourly nomination by the Capacity Holder per ENTSO-E NC CACM procedures, submitted by 12:00 CET D-1.
1.3 "Transmission Rights" — financial or physical transmission rights per the JAO allocation (HAR-1 2025).
1.4 "Use-It-Or-Sell-It (UIOSI)" — the mechanism returning unnominated capacity to the D-1 market; any resale proceeds return pro-rata to Transmission Rights holders.
1.5 "Curtailment" — reduction of scheduled flows due to system security, N-1 reliability, force majeure, or planned maintenance.
1.6 "Congestion Rent" — the price differential between IFA2 price zones (FR NP and UK N2EX) captured on flow schedules.
1.7 "Outage Compensation" — compensation due to Capacity Holder under Cap-and-Floor if interconnector unavailability exceeds the regulatory floor.
1.8 "ITC Fund" — Inter-Transmission System Operator Compensation fund per EU 838/2010.
1.9 "GoO" — Guarantees of Origin per European Directive 2018/2001.
1.10 "Cap-and-Floor Regime" — OFGEM/CRE hybrid revenue mechanism with regulatory floor (£30m/year) and cap (£95m/year).

ARTICLE 2  CAPACITY ALLOCATION
2.1 IFA2 OpCo grants the Capacity Holder the Annual Firm Capacity for the Term.
2.2 Capacity is held on a "use or sell" basis — unnominated capacity flows to UIOSI per Article 8.
2.3 Daily auction participation (DA, ID) operates in parallel — the Capacity Holder's Annual Firm holdings are additive to any daily allocations.
2.4 No right to re-sell the full annual capacity; but intraday resale to other qualified NEMOs is permitted.
2.5 Product substitution: if the JAO closes the FR→UK product, IFA2 OpCo may substitute with UK→FR direction subject to 30-day notice and economic equivalence test.

ARTICLE 3  NOMINATION AND SCHEDULING
3.1 Nominations submitted via JAO platform (e-CMT) daily by 12:00 CET on D-1.
3.2 Scheduling to be consistent with ENTSO-E Coordinated Capacity Calculation Region (CCR Channel) rules.
3.3 Late nominations — rejected automatically by e-CMT. Failure to nominate triggers UIOSI under Article 8.
3.4 Weekly planning: rolling 7-day schedule subject to border control limits as notified by National Grid and RTE.
3.5 Intraday adjustments: permitted per XBID trades; last-updated schedule binds.

ARTICLE 4  ACCESS TO INTRA-DAY MARKETS
4.1 The Capacity Holder has access to XBID (Cross-Border Intra-Day) shared order book for IFA2 capacity resale/re-purchase.
4.2 Continuous intraday trading from D-1 18:00 CET to gate closure (D 00:00 delivery hour).
4.3 Intraday pricing: local NEMO markets (EPEX SPOT, APX) with standard ENTSO-E matching rules.
4.4 XBID failure fallback: auction-based intraday per ENTSO-E Implementation Framework.

ARTICLE 5  COMPENSATION AND FEES
5.1 Annual Capacity Fee: €12.5 million (= €156.25/kW-year × 80 MW), payable monthly in arrears (€1.042m/month).
5.2 Payment is FIRM — not conditional on actual flow, subject only to compensation for Curtailment and Outage per Article 6.
5.3 Indexation: Annual review per HICP-Eurozone published by Eurostat, cap 2.5% pa, floor 0%.
5.4 Congestion rents earned by the Capacity Holder on nominated flows = Σ(hourly flow MW) × (UK price - FR price) — accrue directly to the Capacity Holder on its nominated schedule.
5.5 Auction sweeps: any JAO auction proceeds from UIOSI return to IFA2 OpCo net of 5% administration margin; IFA2 OpCo pays the Capacity Holder 95% of the gross auction-return value within 30 days of settlement.

ARTICLE 6  CURTAILMENT AND OUTAGE COMPENSATION
6.1 Firm Curtailment: capacity reduction below 80 MW nominated FR→UK triggers compensation to the Capacity Holder at the higher of:
     (a) Market spread = max(0, UK_DA_price - FR_DA_price) × Curtailed_MWh, or
     (b) Regulatory floor compensation per OFGEM/CRE methodology v3.2.
6.2 Planned maintenance outages (1-2 per year, each 10-14 days) — pro-rata Capacity Fee reduction, no additional compensation.
6.3 Unplanned outages — compensation starts after 48-hour grace period. Cap: 90 days/year of full compensation.
6.4 Force Majeure events — no compensation for duration of FM event.
6.5 N-1 redispatching (TSO action) — the Capacity Holder compensated at market spread × reduced MWh.

ARTICLE 7  SETTLEMENT
7.1 Daily settlement files via JAO e-CMT — Market Coupling settlement data (MCS) by D+1 14:00 CET.
7.2 Congestion rent settlement: D+3 business days via standard bank transfer.
7.3 Annual reconciliation within 60 days of Contract Year-end.
7.4 Disputes: 20 business days per ENTSO-E dispute resolution framework, escalation to NRA (OFGEM/CRE) if unresolved.

ARTICLE 8  USE-IT-OR-SELL-IT (UIOSI)
8.1 Unnominated capacity returns to D-1 market automatically at gate closure.
8.2 UIOSI proceeds = UIOSI_MWh × (UK_DA - FR_DA, if positive for direction).
8.3 Split: 95% to Capacity Holder, 5% to IFA2 OpCo admin.
8.4 UIOSI revenue does NOT offset the Annual Capacity Fee — the Fee is firm.

ARTICLE 9  CAP-AND-FLOOR REGIME (TSO-level)
9.1 IFA2 OpCo's total ex-Capacity-Fee revenues subject to OFGEM/CRE cap-and-floor:
     Floor: £30.0m/year (approximately €34.5m)
     Cap: £95.0m/year (approximately €109.3m)
9.2 True-ups executed every 5 years against cumulative actual revenues.
9.3 This Agreement is transparent to the cap-and-floor true-up — Annual Capacity Fee flows through to OpCo's revenue.
9.4 Regulatory change (OFGEM RIIO-T2 / CRE TURPE 6) may trigger tariff re-opener per Article 21.

ARTICLE 10  BALANCING AND IMBALANCE
10.1 Balance Responsible Party (BRP): the Capacity Holder is responsible for real-time balancing in both control zones (UK — ESO, France — RTE).
10.2 Imbalance settlement per standard single-price mechanism.
10.3 Deviation alerts: e-CMT issues hourly deviation notices > 50 MW.
10.4 Correction opportunity: intraday trading per Article 4.

ARTICLE 11  PHYSICAL vs FINANCIAL TRANSMISSION RIGHTS
11.1 This Agreement confers Physical Transmission Rights (PTR) with UIOSI.
11.2 Optional conversion to Financial Transmission Rights (FTR): the Capacity Holder may elect FTR conversion at the start of any Contract Year. FTR payoff = price differential × notional × allocated hours, without physical nomination obligations.
11.3 Conversion consequences: loss of physical UIOSI, simplified settlement, smaller market risk.

ARTICLE 12  GREEN CERTIFICATES (GoO)
12.1 Nominated flow volumes carry no automatic GoO transfer — electricity tracked per EU directive as grey/unlabeled.
12.2 Optional Green Corridor service: the Capacity Holder can request certified renewable corridor flows at €1.50/MWh additional fee, requiring GoO match at source (FR nuclear / renewables mix).
12.3 Green Corridor audits: annual by certified verifier (TÜV Rheinland or equivalent).

ARTICLE 13  MARKET MANIPULATION (REMIT COMPLIANCE)
13.1 The Capacity Holder warrants full REMIT compliance, inside information disclosure via UMMs to ACER.
13.2 Trade reporting: via ACER Registered Reporting Mechanism (RRM) within 1 hour of execution.
13.3 Cross-border supervision: ACER / NRAs (OFGEM, CRE) have audit access to all nomination and trading records.

ARTICLE 14  CREDIT SUPPORT
14.1 The Capacity Holder posts bank guarantee equal to 2 months' Annual Capacity Fee = €2.083m.
14.2 Downgrade trigger: Capacity Holder parent rating below BBB- → upgrade to 3 months' collateral + additional €1m cash buffer.
14.3 IFA2 OpCo retains right to suspend service for credit-support breach beyond 15-business-day cure.
14.4 Cross-default: any default under ISDA Master Agreement with either party triggers credit re-review.

ARTICLE 15  FORCE MAJEURE
15.1 Events: storms, marine casualties affecting cable, earthquakes, industrial action beyond 5 days, regulatory suspension, war, sabotage.
15.2 Notification within 12 hours. Detailed particulars within 5 business days.
15.3 Compensation suspension during FM. Cumulative FM > 150 days/year triggers review.
15.4 Termination: either party may terminate after FM > 180 consecutive days.

ARTICLE 16  NORMAL INTERCONNECTOR OUTAGES
16.1 Scheduled: 1-2 windows per year, each max 14 days.
16.2 Scheduling coordination with RTE (French TSO) and National Grid (UK ESO) per SOGL protocols.
16.3 Notice to Capacity Holder: 90 days advance, revised 30 days before, final 7 days before.

ARTICLE 17  CHANGE IN LAW
17.1 Categories:
     (a) Brexit post-transition regulatory frameworks (TCA Title XI Energy).
     (b) NC CACM / NC EB / NC HVDC amendments.
     (c) UK carbon border adjustment mechanism (CBAM) or EU CBAM price application to cross-border flows.
     (d) National Grid / RTE tariff restructuring (TURPE 7, RIIO-T2 Phase 2).
17.2 Material threshold: cumulative >12% of annual economics.
17.3 Re-opener: 90-day window, failing which mutual termination with NPV close-out.

ARTICLE 18  ASSIGNMENT
18.1 Capacity Holder may assign to: Affiliates (no consent), successors in merger/acquisition (no consent).
18.2 Third-party assignment: IFA2 OpCo consent (not unreasonably withheld) + credit support transfer.
18.3 JAO rules require secondary market auction-based transfer for certain periods.

ARTICLE 19  BUSINESS CONTINUITY
19.1 Both parties maintain ISO 22301 business continuity certifications.
19.2 Annual BCP test including failover of nomination systems.
19.3 Data retention: 10 years for nomination/settlement records per REMIT.

ARTICLE 20  CYBERSECURITY
20.1 NIS2 compliance (EU Directive 2022/2555) + UK NIS Regulations.
20.2 Annual ISO 27001 certification or SOC 2 Type II.
20.3 Incident notification: 24 hours for potential breaches, detailed within 72 hours.

ARTICLE 21  RE-OPENER / MATERIAL ADVERSE CHANGE
21.1 Triggers: Article 17 changes of law; >15% change in forward cross-border spread for >6 consecutive months; regulatory disqualification of either party.
21.2 Window: 60 days good-faith, escalation to CEO-level within 30 days, arbitration if still unresolved.

ARTICLE 22  TERMINATION
22.1 Events of Default:
     (a) Payment >30 days overdue.
     (b) Insolvency, administration, CVA.
     (c) Regulatory disqualification (NRA revocation, ACER ban).
     (d) Credit support breach >15 business days.
22.2 Cure: (a) 15 business days; others no cure.
22.3 Termination Payment: NPV of remaining Capacity Fee + NPV of lost congestion rent (forward curve basis), discounted at Euribor + 300 bps.

ARTICLE 23  DISPUTE RESOLUTION
23.1 Step 1: Negotiation (20 business days).
23.2 Step 2: ACER/NRA mediation (if regulatory-interpretation issue).
23.3 Step 3: LCIA arbitration, London, English law for contractual matters / French law for TSO regulatory matters (dual-regime).
23.4 Emergency relief: court injunction permitted for cyber incidents or sanctions issues.

ARTICLE 24  REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
24.1 Mutual: authority, licenses, no known proceedings.
24.2 IFA2 OpCo: valid OFGEM/CRE licence, ENTSO-E system operator compliance.
24.3 Capacity Holder: EPEX SPOT + APX membership, ENTSO-E BRP qualification, REMIT registration, full regulatory standing.

ARTICLE 25  CONFIDENTIALITY
25.1 Confidentiality: 5 years post-termination, excluding regulatory / statutory disclosures.
25.2 REMIT — mandatory regulatory disclosures permitted.
25.3 Trade data aggregation for ACER Market Monitoring permitted.

ARTICLE 26  NOTICES
26.1 Formal: email + registered post.
