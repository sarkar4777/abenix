================================================================================
            DORE BAR INTAKE AND REFINING AGREEMENT
                  (Long-form Off-take from Mine to Refiner)

                          CONTRACT NO. PMR-2026-014

                      DATE OF EXECUTION: 18 February 2026
================================================================================

BETWEEN:

(1) ANDEAN PRIMA MINING S.A., a corporation duly incorporated under the laws
    of the Republic of Peru, with its registered office at Avenida Javier
    Prado Este 4990, San Borja, Lima 41, Peru, registered with the National
    Superintendency of Public Registries (SUNARP) under entry number
    11420382, RUC 20489372617 (the "Seller" or "Mine");

(2) HELVETIA METALS REFINING AG, a stock corporation (Aktiengesellschaft)
    duly incorporated under the laws of the Swiss Confederation, with its
    registered office at Bahnhofstrasse 18, 8001 Zurich, Switzerland,
    registered in the Commercial Register of the Canton of Zurich under
    CHE-104.582.913, holding a current Good Delivery listing from the London
    Bullion Market Association in respect of Gold and Silver (the "Refiner").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Mine produces a primary gold-silver concentrate at its operations in
    the Yauli district, Junin region of Peru, and refines it on-site into
    semi-refined dore bars of nominal composition seventy-five per cent
    (75%) gold (Au) and twenty per cent (20%) silver (Ag), with the balance
    being copper, zinc and trace base metals.

(B) The Refiner operates a Good Delivery-listed refinery at Mendrisio, in
    the Swiss canton of Ticino, and is subject to supervision by the Swiss
    Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA) and to the Swiss Precious
    Metals Control Act (PMCA).

(C) The parties wish to record the terms under which the Mine shall sell and
    the Refiner shall purchase the Mine's production of dore bars for the
    twenty-four (24) month term beginning on the Effective Date.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MATERIAL AND BAR SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Material. The material delivered hereunder shall be dore bars of nominal
    composition Au 750 - Ag 200 - other 50 (parts per thousand). The Seller
    shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain a minimum gold
    fineness of nine hundred and fifty parts per thousand (0.950) on a
    monthly weighted-average basis across all bars delivered.

1.2 Bar form. Each dore bar shall be cast in the Seller's licensed casting
    facility, sequentially serialised, and stamped with the Seller's
    refinery mark, the lot number, the gross weight in troy ounces, and the
    estimated Au and Ag content.

1.3 Bar count and tonnage. The Seller shall deliver between three thousand
    five hundred (3,500) and four thousand five hundred (4,500) troy ounces
    of gold-equivalent content per calendar month. The contract minimum for
    the twenty-four (24) month term is eighty-four thousand (84,000) troy
    ounces gold-equivalent. The contract maximum is one hundred and eight
    thousand (108,000) troy ounces gold-equivalent.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RESPONSIBLE SOURCING AND COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 LBMA Responsible Gold Guidance. The Refiner is subject to the London
    Bullion Market Association Responsible Gold Guidance, version 9 of
    December 2023 (the "RGG"), and shall maintain its Good Delivery listing
    throughout the term. The Seller represents and warrants that:

    (a) it has a documented supply chain policy aligned with the OECD Due
        Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from
        Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas (3rd edition, 2016) (the
        "OECD DDG");

    (b) it has conducted a documented risk assessment of its supply chain
        and shall make such records available to the Refiner upon request;

    (c) it has implemented and maintains a corrective-action plan
        consistent with Step 3 of the OECD DDG; and

    (d) it shall procure and provide to the Refiner an annual independent
        third-party audit report consistent with Step 4 of the OECD DDG,
        no later than 31 March of each calendar year.

2.2 OECD 5-Step. The parties acknowledge that the Refiner shall report on
    supply chain due diligence in accordance with OECD Step 5 in its
    annual public disclosure and in the LBMA-mandated annual Compliance
    Report. The Seller agrees to provide all reasonable cooperation.

2.3 Chain of integrity. Each dore bar shall be delivered with a serialised
    Chain-of-Custody record. The Refiner reserves the right to track every
    bar in its enterprise vault-management system from gate-in through to
    investment-bar casting, and to provide the Mine with downstream
    serialised reports on the products derived from each lot.

2.4 RJC. The Refiner holds a current Responsible Jewellery Council Chain of
    Custody certificate (RJC CoC certificate number to be provided
    separately). The Seller shall furnish, on request, evidence sufficient
    for the Refiner to maintain its RJC CoC certification.

2.5 Excluded origins. The Seller represents that no material delivered
    hereunder originates from, or has been processed in, the Russian
    Federation, the Republic of Belarus, the Democratic People's Republic
    of Korea, the Islamic Republic of Iran, the Republic of the Sudan, the
    Democratic Republic of the Congo, or any other jurisdiction that is at
    any time the subject of comprehensive sanctions imposed by the United
    States Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), the European Union, or
    the United Nations.

2.6 Section 1502. Where the Refiner's downstream customer is a person
    subject to Section 1502 of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and
    Consumer Protection Act, the Seller shall execute the Refiner's
    standard Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT) on request.

2.7 ISO. The Refiner shall maintain certification under ISO 9001:2015 and
    ISO 14001:2015. Loss of either certification shall constitute a
    material breach by the Refiner.

2.8 Swiss PMCA. The Refiner shall complete every required filing under the
    Swiss Precious Metals Control Act, including the per-import origin
    declaration filed with the Swiss Federal Customs Administration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DELIVERY AND LOCO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Loco. All deliveries shall be made loco Zurich, free into the vault of
    the Refiner at Mendrisio, Switzerland.

3.2 Logistics. The Seller shall arrange bonded transport from the Mine to
    the Lima Jorge Chavez International Airport and onward air freight to
    Zurich-Kloten with an LBMA-recognised secure-logistics carrier.

3.3 Insurance. The Seller shall procure all-risk in-transit insurance from
    Mine gate to Refiner vault gate, with a minimum coverage of one hundred
    and ten per cent (110%) of the contractual value of the consignment.
    The Refiner shall be a named additional insured.

3.4 Delivery window. The standard delivery cadence is one (1) shipment per
    calendar month. The Seller shall provide ten (10) calendar days'
    advance notice of each shipment.

3.5 Late-delivery. Any shipment delivered more than fifteen (15) calendar
    days after the notified delivery date shall constitute a "Late
    Delivery". The Refiner may, at its option, charge a late-delivery fee
    of one quarter of one per cent (0.25%) of the consignment value per
    additional week of delay, or terminate the affected delivery and claim
    the difference between the contract price and the then-current LBMA
    Gold Fix.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ASSAY AND WEIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Assay protocol. On gate-in at the Refiner, each bar shall be weighed
    and assayed by the Refiner's assay laboratory using fire assay for
    gold and ICP-OES for silver. The Refiner's assay shall be communicated
    to the Seller within five (5) business days of gate-in.

4.2 Split-sample. The Seller shall be entitled to require a split-sample
    procedure on any individual bar. Each party shall retain one sample;
    the third sample shall be sealed and held by the Refiner for
    independent assay at an umpire laboratory should a dispute arise.

4.3 Assay tolerance. Where the Refiner's reported gold fineness differs
    from the Seller's stated fineness by more than five hundredths of one
    per cent (0.05%) on an absolute basis, the parties shall jointly
    instruct an umpire laboratory listed on Schedule B. The umpire's
    assay shall be binding. The party whose assay was more incorrect, by
    absolute deviation from the umpire result, shall bear the umpire
    laboratory's costs.

4.4 Weight. Each bar shall be weighed to one one-hundredth (0.01) of a
    troy ounce. Fine weight is computed as gross weight multiplied by
    fineness divided by one thousand. Contract price applies to fine
    weight only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PRICING AND PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Reference price. The price payable for each troy ounce of fine gold
    delivered hereunder shall be:

       Gold settlement price = (LBMA Gold PM Fix on the Pricing Date)
                               minus the Treatment Charge per troy ounce
                               minus the Refining Charge per troy ounce

    where the Treatment Charge is fixed at four United States dollars
    (USD 4.00) per troy ounce of fine gold and the Refining Charge is
    fixed at one United States dollar and fifty cents (USD 1.50) per troy
    ounce of fine gold.

5.2 Silver settlement price = (LBMA Silver Price on the Pricing Date)
    minus a Refining Charge of twenty-five United States cents (USD 0.25)
    per troy ounce of fine silver, with payable fixed at ninety-five per
    cent (95%) of the contained silver.

5.3 Pricing Date. The Pricing Date shall be the second business day
    following gate-in of the relevant consignment, unless either party
    elects in writing to defer pricing for up to thirty (30) calendar
    days from gate-in.

5.4 Provisional payment. The Refiner shall make a provisional payment
    equal to ninety per cent (90%) of the estimated settlement value
    within five (5) business days of gate-in, computed against the
    Seller's stated fineness. The balance shall be paid within ten (10)
    business days following final assay.

5.5 Settlement currency. All payments shall be in United States Dollars
    (USD), by wire transfer to the bank account designated by the Seller
    in writing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. VAULTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 At and from gate-in, the dore bars become the property of the Refiner
    and form part of the Refiner's unallocated working inventory. The
    Seller acknowledges that no segregated or allocated vault account is
    maintained in respect of unsettled consignments hereunder.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. TERM, TERMINATION AND FORCE MAJEURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on 1 March 2026 (the "Effective
    Date") and continue for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months,
    automatically renewing for successive twelve-month periods unless
    terminated by either party on not less than ninety (90) days' written
    notice prior to expiration.

7.2 Good Delivery list status. If the Refiner is suspended or removed
    from the LBMA Good Delivery List for Gold or Silver during the term,
    the Seller may, on five (5) business days' written notice, terminate
    this Agreement without liability and direct any in-transit shipment
    to an alternative LBMA-listed refinery on the Refiner's account.

7.3 Force majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure
    arising from acts of God, war, government action, pandemic, or other
    causes beyond the affected party's reasonable control. The affected
    party shall give prompt notice. Performance shall be suspended for
    the duration of the event up to a maximum of ninety (90) calendar
    days, after which either party may terminate the affected shipments.

7.4 Sanctions termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement on
    written notice if continued performance would breach the sanctions
    regime of the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom,
    Switzerland or the United Nations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. GENERAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the substantive law of
    Switzerland, excluding its conflict of laws rules and the United
    Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods.

8.2 Dispute resolution. Disputes shall be finally settled by arbitration
    in Zurich under the Swiss Rules of International Arbitration, by a
    panel of three arbitrators, in English.

8.3 Confidentiality. The terms of this Agreement, all assay reports, and
    all chain-of-custody records are confidential between the parties.

8.4 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.
