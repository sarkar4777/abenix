BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM (BESS) TOLLING AND GRID SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Texas ERCOT 400 MW / 800 MWh Facility — Brazos Bend BESS)

BETWEEN
(1) BRAZOS BEND STORAGE LLC — "Owner"
    a Delaware limited liability company having its principal office at 1001 Fannin Street, Suite 3000, Houston, Texas 77002.
(2) ATLANTIC RENEWABLES TRADING, LLC — "Tolling Counterparty"
    a Delaware limited liability company having its principal office at 825 Third Avenue, New York, NY 10022.

EXECUTION DATE: 22 March 2025
COMMERCIAL OPERATION DATE (COD) TARGET: 1 October 2026
TERM: Fifteen (15) Years from COD with 2 × 5-year extensions
FACILITY: 400 MW / 800 MWh 4-hour Lithium-Iron-Phosphate BESS
INTERCONNECTION: Skyview 345 kV GTC substation (ERCOT nodal LZ_HOUSTON)
CONTRACT REFERENCE: BBBESS-TOL-2025-HOU-4404

RECITALS
WHEREAS the Owner has developed the BESS and holds the ERCOT interconnection agreement;
WHEREAS the Tolling Counterparty wishes to secure 100% dispatch rights to the Facility for energy arbitrage, ancillary services and capacity market participation;
WHEREAS the Parties have agreed the terms and conditions for tolling, operations, performance guarantees and economics below.

ARTICLE 1  DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Availability" — fraction of time the Facility is available for dispatch, excluding Force Majeure and scheduled maintenance per Section 1.1 of Schedule 3.
1.2 "Capacity Fee" — USD/kW-month, payable monthly as per Article 6.
1.3 "Cycle" — one full charge-discharge event equivalent to 1.0 × nameplate energy throughput (800 MWh).
1.4 "Degradation Curve" — the warranted round-trip efficiency and capacity retention schedule in Schedule 2.
1.5 "Dispatch Instructions" — instructions from the Tolling Counterparty submitted via OASIS API for day-ahead and real-time dispatch.
1.6 "Energy Charge Rate" — USD/MWh charged for charging electricity, equal to the ERCOT LMP at LZ_HOUSTON.
1.7 "Grid Services" — AS products: Regulation Up (RegUp), Regulation Down (RegDn), Responsive Reserve (RRS), ECRS, Non-Spin.
1.8 "Performance Guarantee" — the round-trip efficiency (85%) and availability (96%) thresholds in Article 10.
1.9 "Tolling Fee" — the fixed monthly Capacity Fee plus the variable Performance Bonus per Article 6.4.
1.10 "Capacity Retention" — the Usable Energy Capacity expressed as a percentage of nameplate, tested per Article 8.

ARTICLE 2  DISPATCH RIGHTS
2.1 The Tolling Counterparty has exclusive right to dispatch 100% of the Facility's Available Capacity for the Term.
2.2 Dispatch instructions via OASIS API with 5-minute granularity. Real-time operator cover: 24/7.
2.3 The Tolling Counterparty may co-optimise across Day-Ahead Energy, Real-Time Energy, AS products, and ERCOT capacity auction participation.

ARTICLE 3  OWNER'S OPERATIONAL OBLIGATIONS
3.1 Owner to maintain the Facility per OEM specifications (Tesla Megapack 2XL, firmware version NUC-2024.3 minimum).
3.2 Preventive maintenance: annual full inspection (14 days), quarterly HVAC+fire system checks (2 days each).
3.3 Warranty administration: Owner is primary warranty holder; all claims coordinated through Owner.
3.4 Augmentation obligation: Owner shall add capacity to maintain 100% nameplate over Years 1-8 (estimated 4-6 augmentation events, cost-shared per Article 14).

ARTICLE 4  METERING AND PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT
4.1 ERCOT Qualified Scheduling Entity (QSE) role: Owner-provided QSE, transitioning to Tolling Counterparty's QSE at COD+12 months.
4.2 Metering: ERCOT-approved revenue meters, ANSI C12.20 Class 0.2. Quarterly calibration.
4.3 Round-trip efficiency (RTE): measured monthly as (total AC discharge energy) / (total AC charge energy) over ≥100 full cycles.
4.4 State of Charge (SoC): maintained between 10% and 95% per augmentation strategy; Owner alarms at bounds.

ARTICLE 5  CYCLING LIMITS AND WARRANTY
5.1 Maximum allowed cycles: 365 full equivalent cycles/year (cumulative), tracked by Owner SCADA.
5.2 Cycles above 365 trigger additional degradation charges: USD 9,500 per excess cycle (paid by Tolling Counterparty to Owner).
5.3 Tesla battery warranty: 20 years / 70% capacity retention under the agreed cycle profile.
5.4 Operating temperature: ambient -20°C to +50°C; cell-level 0-45°C.

ARTICLE 6  COMMERCIAL TERMS
6.1 Capacity Fee: USD 8.50 / kW-month (2025 real), applied to nameplate 400,000 kW = USD 3,400,000/month = USD 40,800,000/year (Year 1).
6.2 Indexation: Annual, CPI-U cap 3% floor 0%.
6.3 Performance Bonus: If monthly Availability > 97% AND RTE > 86%, Tolling Counterparty pays Owner USD 0.50/kW-month bonus. Capped at 24 months.
6.4 Performance Penalty: Monthly Availability < 94% → Capacity Fee reduction at USD 0.80/kW-month per percentage-point deficiency, up to USD 5.00/kW-month max.
6.5 Augmentation Cost Recovery: capital augmentation cost passed through at 110% (10% Owner margin), amortised over 8 years remaining term.

ARTICLE 7  PAYMENT TERMS
7.1 Capacity Fee payable monthly in arrears, invoice on 5th business day.
7.2 Charging energy reimbursed by Tolling Counterparty at actual ERCOT LMP plus 0.5% admin fee.
7.3 Net settlement: monthly net invoice covering Capacity Fee + augmentation + penalties - performance bonuses.
7.4 Payment due: 15 days from invoice. Late interest: Prime Rate + 200 bps.

ARTICLE 8  CAPACITY WARRANTY AND TESTING
8.1 Annual capacity test: discharge from 95% SoC to 10% SoC at 100 MW (C/4) constant power, measure delivered kWh at POD.
8.2 Year 1 guaranteed Usable Capacity: 780 MWh (97.5% of nameplate).
8.3 Capacity retention schedule: Year 5 ≥ 88%, Year 10 ≥ 78%, Year 15 ≥ 70%.
8.4 Failure to meet retention: Liquidated Damages of USD 150 per kWh shortfall, or Owner funded augmentation within 90 days.

ARTICLE 9  GRID SERVICES REVENUE SHARING
9.1 AS revenue (RegUp, RegDn, RRS, ECRS, Non-Spin) and energy margin fully retained by Tolling Counterparty (tolling structure).
9.2 No gain-share on energy or AS revenue to Owner except Performance Bonus in Article 6.3.
9.3 Capacity market (ERCOT VRR, if implemented) revenue: 80% Tolling Counterparty / 20% Owner.
9.4 Black Start service (if Facility qualifies): fixed fee USD 150,000/month, 50/50 split.

ARTICLE 10  PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES
10.1 Availability Guarantee: 96.0% annual, measured as (Available Hours) / (8,760 - Scheduled Maintenance Hours - FM Hours).
10.2 Round-Trip Efficiency: 85% at standard test conditions (Schedule 2 §4), measured monthly.
10.3 Response Time: 100% rated output within 1 second of AGC signal receipt.
10.4 Failure of 10.1 or 10.2 triggers monthly Capacity Fee reduction per Article 6.4.

ARTICLE 11  DEGRADATION MANAGEMENT AND AUGMENTATION
11.1 Owner shall maintain Usable Energy Capacity ≥ 88% of nameplate through Year 8 via cell augmentation.
11.2 Augmentation Events: anticipated in Years 3, 5, 7 (cumulative 12-16 additional megapacks).
11.3 Augmentation cost pass-through capped at USD 38M aggregate over the Term.
11.4 Tolling Counterparty consent required before augmentation > USD 8M single event.

ARTICLE 12  REGULATORY AND INTERCONNECTION
12.1 Owner maintains ERCOT Interconnection Agreement and all applicable NERC, TRE, and PUCT filings.
12.2 Change of Law: material regulatory changes (e.g. ERCOT Real-Time Co-Optimization Phase 2, capacity market introduction) trigger re-opener in Article 22.
12.3 IRA tax credits (ITC 30% + 10% domestic content) remain with Owner; Tolling Counterparty reimburses 15% as a tax equity benefit flow-through in the Capacity Fee.

ARTICLE 13  CHANGE IN LAW
13.1 Material change: cumulative impact > 7% of annual Capacity Fee.
13.2 Renegotiation window: 120 days; if no agreement, arbitration per Article 24.
13.3 Indirect policy changes (e.g., IRA rollback, ERCOT CAP market) trigger mandatory good-faith review.

ARTICLE 14  CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND IMPROVEMENTS
14.1 Owner-funded CapEx: initial construction, end-of-life decommissioning, all scheduled augmentations (subject to Article 11.3 cap).
14.2 Tolling Counterparty CapEx: discretionary controls upgrades, EMS/SCADA integrations with its trading systems, fiber interconnection to its trading floor.

ARTICLE 15  INSURANCE
15.1 Owner: All-risk property (USD 450M), GL (USD 25M/occ), Business Interruption (USD 75M / 12 months), Environmental Liability (USD 15M).
15.2 Tolling Counterparty: GL (USD 10M/occ), Errors & Omissions (USD 25M aggregate).
15.3 Waiver of subrogation mutual for property and GL.

ARTICLE 16  FORCE MAJEURE
16.1 Events: natural disasters (hurricane, earthquake, flooding, wildfire), war, terrorism, cyber-attacks, ERCOT-declared emergency ops, ISO/RTO dispatch restrictions, strikes beyond 7 days.
16.2 Notice: 48 hours. Regular updates every 5 days.
16.3 Suspension: Capacity Fee suspended pro-rata during FM outage > 72 hours.
16.4 Termination right: FM duration > 270 consecutive days.

ARTICLE 17  CREDIT SUPPORT AND COLLATERAL
17.1 Initial collateral: Tolling Counterparty provides parent guarantee (Atlantic Renewables, Inc. — S&P BBB+) equal to 6 months Capacity Fee = USD 20.4M.
17.2 Ongoing threshold: MtM monitored monthly; additional margin if exposure > USD 10M.
17.3 Owner collateral: Letter of Credit USD 15M to secure performance guarantees in Article 10.
17.4 Downgrade triggers: either party falling below BBB- → 20-day cure to enhance collateral.

ARTICLE 18  CYBERSECURITY
18.1 NERC CIP-005/007/009/011 compliance (critical cyber assets).
18.2 Dispatch API hardened: TLS 1.3 mutual auth, IP allowlisting.
18.3 Annual cyber audit by SOC 2 Type II auditor, shared report.
18.4 Incident notification: 24 hours for suspected, 72 hours for confirmed breaches per NERC CIP-008.

ARTICLE 19  CONFIDENTIALITY
19.1 All commercial terms confidential for 5 years post-termination.
19.2 Regulatory / legal disclosures permitted.
19.3 Performance data shared with OEM (Tesla) for warranty analytics.

ARTICLE 20  END OF LIFE / DECOMMISSIONING
20.1 Decommissioning Fund: Owner maintains USD 5M escrow, replenished annually.
20.2 At end of term, Owner responsible for decommissioning, recycling per EPA e-waste regulations, site restoration.
20.3 Residual value: any salvage revenue to Owner.

ARTICLE 21  GOVERNANCE AND OPS COMMITTEE
21.1 Joint Operating Committee (JOC): 4 members (2 each party), quarterly meetings.
21.2 JOC reviews: performance data, upcoming maintenance, augmentation, market outlook.
21.3 Escalation: unresolved items → CEO-level within 15 days.

ARTICLE 22  RE-OPENER AND RENEGOTIATION
22.1 Contractual review at Year 5 and Year 10 (mutual, not mandatory).
22.2 Material Adverse Change: cumulative >15% NPV degradation to either party.
22.3 Timeline: 90-day negotiation; failure → arbitration.

ARTICLE 23  TERMINATION
23.1 Events of Default:
     (a) Failure to pay > 30 days.
     (b) Insolvency, Chapter 11 filing, receivership.
     (c) Failure to meet Availability < 85% over 6 months.
     (d) Sanctions / regulatory disqualification.
23.2 Cure periods: (a) 30 days; (c) 180 days; no cure for (b) or (d).
23.3 Termination Payment: NPV of remaining Capacity Fee losses - NPV of replacement toll opportunity, discounted at 10%.

ARTICLE 24  DISPUTE RESOLUTION
24.1 Mediation first (30 days). Then JAMS arbitration in Houston, Texas.
24.2 Governing law: Texas, excluding conflicts of laws.
24.3 Venue exclusive: federal/state courts sitting in Harris County, Texas.

ARTICLE 25  REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
25.1 Mutual: authority, no conflicts, no known material adverse proceedings.
25.2 Owner: title to Facility, valid interconnection agreement, environmental permits, NERC registration.
25.3 Tolling Counterparty: QSE qualification (or ability to procure), trading floor authority.
