VIRTUAL POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (VPPA) — FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT
Hyperion Corporation (Buyer) and Arroyo Solar III LLC (Seller)
Solar PV Facility: 450 MW DC / 350 MW AC — West Texas (ERCOT West Hub)

EXECUTION DATE: 30 November 2024
COMMERCIAL OPERATION DATE TARGET (COD): 15 September 2026
TERM: Fifteen (15) Years from COD
CONTRACTED CAPACITY: 300 MW AC (Seller's 450/350 MW total, ~85.7%)
SETTLEMENT HUB: ERCOT West Hub
CfD STRUCTURE: Two-way financial settlement (no physical delivery)
REFERENCE: HYP-VPPA-2024-0312

PREAMBLE
This Virtual Power Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is a pure financial contract for differences between Hyperion Corporation ("Buyer") — a Delaware technology corporation — and Arroyo Solar III LLC ("Seller") — a Delaware limited liability company and the owner of the Facility. The Buyer does not take physical delivery of electricity. Payments settle the difference between a fixed Strike Price and a floating Market Price, consistent with the Environmental Attributes transfer provisions in Article 8.

ARTICLE 1  DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Contracted Capacity" — 300 MW AC, the portion of the Facility's output included in this VPPA.
1.2 "Expected Annual Generation" — 792 GWh/year (P50), based on solar resource and technology parameters.
1.3 "Strike Price" — USD 42.85/MWh (2024 real), indexed per Article 7.
1.4 "Floating Price" — ERCOT West Hub hourly Real-Time Locational Marginal Price (LMP).
1.5 "Settlement Quantity" — Metered generation at POI × (Contracted Capacity / Total Facility Capacity) = Metered Output × 85.7%.
1.6 "Environmental Attributes" — all Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs), Green-e certifications, GHG emissions avoidance, any Inflation Reduction Act PTC-related benefits, and like attributes.
1.7 "Shape Basis Risk" — the difference between hub prices and nodal prices at the POI.
1.8 "Curtailment" — scheduled or economic curtailment by ERCOT or the Seller.
1.9 "Settlement Period" — hourly, aligned with ERCOT Real-Time intervals.
1.10 "Negative Pricing Event" — any hour with Floating Price < USD 0.

ARTICLE 2  STRUCTURE: FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT ONLY
2.1 This is a virtual PPA: no physical power flows from Seller to Buyer.
2.2 Seller sells physical output to ERCOT wholesale market at floating prices.
2.3 Parties settle the CfD hourly, with Buyer paying Seller (Strike - Float) × Settlement Quantity when Strike > Float, and Seller paying Buyer (Float - Strike) × Settlement Quantity when Float > Strike.
2.4 Environmental Attributes transfer from Seller to Buyer per Article 8.

ARTICLE 3  COMMERCIAL OPERATION DATE (COD)
3.1 Target COD: 15 September 2026.
3.2 Guaranteed Commercial Operation Date (GCOD): 30 June 2027 (Longstop).
3.3 COD Requirements: ≥95% Mechanical Completion, Initial Synchronisation, successful 72-hour stability test, all required permits.
3.4 Delay Liquidated Damages: USD 15,000/day past Target COD, capped at USD 2.5M; termination right at GCOD with USD 15M damages.
3.5 Early COD: Buyer may elect early start within 30 days of Seller's declaration.

ARTICLE 4  TERM AND TERMINATION
4.1 Initial Term: 15 years from COD. No automatic renewal.
4.2 Early termination: mutual only, with NPV close-out.

ARTICLE 5  METERING AND MEASUREMENT
5.1 Settlement meters: ANSI C12.20 Class 0.2, at the Point of Interconnection (POI) at the 345 kV substation.
5.2 ERCOT-registered: Seller-operated, Buyer-audited annually.
5.3 Data source: ERCOT Settlement-Quality Meter Data (SQMD), 15-minute intervals aggregated hourly.
5.4 Measurement disputes: resolved within 30 days per ERCOT Nodal Protocols §10.

ARTICLE 6  PRICING AND STRIKE MECHANICS
6.1 Base Strike Price: USD 42.85/MWh (2024 real dollars).
6.2 Annual Indexation: 1.0% fixed escalator (not CPI-linked), applied January 1 each year.
6.3 Formula per hour h:
     Net Payment = (Strike(h) - Float(h)) × Settlement_Quantity(h)
     — positive: Buyer pays Seller
     — negative: Seller pays Buyer
6.4 Monthly settlement: net of all hours in the calendar month.
6.5 Minimum Strike Floor: USD 35/MWh (protects Seller in deflationary scenario).
6.6 Maximum Strike Cap: USD 65/MWh (protects Buyer from extreme escalation).

ARTICLE 7  NEGATIVE PRICING AND CURTAILMENT RULES
7.1 Negative Pricing Event: when Float Price < USD 0, the CfD payment from Buyer to Seller is capped at USD 0 (no payment from Buyer). Seller bears the negative price risk.
7.2 Curtailment events: Settlement Quantity excludes curtailed hours regardless of cause.
7.3 Grid-caused curtailment > 150 hours/year in any 12-month period triggers good-faith review (re-opener) under Article 21.
7.4 Nodal-to-Hub basis: if ERCOT West Hub minus POI nodal price exceeds USD 5/MWh for > 20% of hours in any quarter, basis-risk review under Article 21.

ARTICLE 8  ENVIRONMENTAL ATTRIBUTES (RECs, IRA benefits)
8.1 Title transfer: all RECs and Environmental Attributes transfer from Seller to Buyer upon ERCOT generation registration.
8.2 Delivery method: via Green-e-certified tracking system (ERCOT WREGIS if applicable, or MRETS).
8.3 Annual true-up: delivered MWh × 1 REC per MWh. Shortfall triggers replacement REC purchase by Seller from qualifying Texas solar sources.
8.4 IRA ITC benefits: Seller retains 30% base ITC + 10% domestic content adders. Buyer receives PTC value via Strike Price reduction (USD 25/MWh effective PTC value for Year 1, stepping down).
8.5 Net zero reporting: Seller provides annual Scope 2 avoided emissions report per GHG Protocol.

ARTICLE 9  PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT
9.1 Payment frequency: monthly, net settlement.
9.2 Invoice: Seller issues within 10 business days of month-end.
9.3 Due date: 20 calendar days from invoice.
9.4 Late interest: Federal Funds + 250 bps.
9.5 Dispute resolution: 30-day review period; undisputed portions paid when due.
9.6 Setoff: Buyer may setoff against Seller's owed amounts from this or any other agreement.

ARTICLE 10  BUYER CREDIT SUPPORT
10.1 Buyer is Hyperion Corporation — Moody's Aa3 / S&P AA-. No ongoing credit support required while Buyer maintains investment grade (A3/A- or better).
10.2 Rating downgrade trigger: below A3/A-:
     (a) Provide parent guarantee within 10 business days, or
     (b) Letter of Credit equal to 6 months maximum exposure (estimated USD 15M), or
     (c) Prepayment of 3 months.
10.3 Cross-default: default under USD 50M+ obligations elsewhere triggers review.

ARTICLE 11  SELLER CREDIT SUPPORT
11.1 Seller's parent Arroyo Renewables Inc. (S&P BBB) provides Parent Company Guarantee equal to USD 25M.
11.2 Replacement: if PCG provider downgraded below BBB-, Seller has 20 days to provide (a) upgrade, (b) substitute investment-grade guarantor, or (c) cash collateral USD 15M.
11.3 Tax equity partner (pre-COD): Seller has tax-equity partnership with Bank of America Renewable Energy Group. Partnership flip date: COD + 60 months. Partner change requires Buyer consent.

ARTICLE 12  FORCE MAJEURE
12.1 Events: natural disaster, force majeure weather (hurricanes, ice storms per ERCOT protocols), ERCOT-declared emergency suspensions, strikes > 14 days, change in law preventing performance.
12.2 NOT Force Majeure: ordinary business failures, insufficient insolation / merchant market conditions, routine maintenance.
12.3 Notification: 48 hours, with 10-day particulars.
12.4 Suspension: CfD settlement suspended during FM > 72 hours.
12.5 Termination right: FM > 365 consecutive days.

ARTICLE 13  CHANGE IN LAW
13.1 Categories:
     (a) Direct impact on RECs (e.g., federal REC scheme).
     (b) ERCOT protocol changes materially affecting Floating Price.
     (c) IRA tax credit structural changes.
     (d) Carbon pricing introduction (e.g., federal or ERCOT carbon adder).
13.2 Materiality threshold: >10% NPV impact.
13.3 Re-opener: 90-day good-faith negotiation; if no agreement, bilateral termination with NPV close-out at current Strike/Float forward curves.

ARTICLE 14  REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
14.1 Mutual: authority, no conflicts.
14.2 Seller: title to Facility, interconnection agreement in good standing, all permits (EPC, environmental, ERCOT registration).
14.3 Buyer: no regulatory disqualification for this VPPA structure.

ARTICLE 15  DISCLAIMER OF PHYSICAL DELIVERY
15.1 Buyer acknowledges no physical delivery — this is a pure financial hedge.
15.2 Buyer not responsible for market scheduling, load shape, dispatch, or ERCOT nodal pricing risk beyond basis risk in Article 7.
15.3 Buyer's electricity supply sourced separately from retail provider / self-supply; this Agreement is for GHG / cost hedging purposes only.

ARTICLE 16  INSURANCE
16.1 Seller: Property (USD 400M All-Risk), Business Interruption (USD 100M), GL (USD 25M/occ), Environmental (USD 10M), Cyber (USD 10M).
16.2 Buyer: D&O + GL per normal corporate insurance.

ARTICLE 17  NO ADJUSTMENT FOR CURTAILMENT COMPENSATION
17.1 Any compensation from ERCOT (e.g., Constrained-Off make-whole) for curtailed hours belongs to Seller.
17.2 No adjustment required under the VPPA formula — Seller's merchant revenue already reflects ERCOT make-whole provisions.

ARTICLE 18  TAX AND ACCOUNTING
18.1 Seller: responsible for all ITC/PTC filings, sales taxes, property taxes.
18.2 Buyer: records the VPPA as a derivative under ASC 815 (hedge accounting), or per FASB cash-flow hedge treatment.
18.3 Notify other party of any material tax position changes within 30 days.

ARTICLE 19  ASSIGNMENT
19.1 Seller may assign to: a subsidiary (no consent), a tax-equity partner exercising flip rights (no consent), or a creditworthy third party (Buyer consent).
19.2 Buyer may assign to: any Buyer Affiliate (no consent), a successor in a merger/acquisition (no consent), or a third-party investor (Seller consent).
19.3 Security interest: Seller may grant first-lien to Project Lenders via direct agreement; Buyer acknowledges lender cure rights.

ARTICLE 20  DISPUTE RESOLUTION
20.1 Step 1: Good-faith negotiation (30 days).
20.2 Step 2: Senior-management escalation (20 days).
20.3 Step 3: JAMS arbitration in Austin, Texas under Energy Industry Rules.
20.4 Governing law: Texas, excluding conflicts of laws.
20.5 Confidentiality applies to all steps.

ARTICLE 21  RE-OPENER / MATERIAL ADVERSE CHANGE
21.1 Scheduled reviews: Years 5 and 10 (optional mutual).
21.2 Unscheduled re-opener triggers:
     (a) Grid curtailment > 150 hours/year for 2 consecutive years.
     (b) Basis (nodal-hub) > USD 5/MWh for 4 consecutive quarters.
     (c) Cumulative IRA / REC policy changes > USD 10/MWh NPV impact.
21.3 Re-opener window: 60 days to mutually agree; failure triggers close-out with 45-day termination notice.

ARTICLE 22  TERMINATION AND CLOSE-OUT
22.1 Events of Default:
     (a) Payment > 30 days overdue (cure 10 days).
     (b) Insolvency (no cure).
     (c) Credit support breach > 15 business days.
     (d) Sanctions / regulatory disqualification.
22.2 Close-out payment: NPV of expected future settlements discounted at the weighted Strike/Float forward curve + USD 8.5% discount.
22.3 Setoff with other obligations permitted.
