POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT
HYBRID OFFSHORE WIND + GUARANTEES OF ORIGIN PROJECT
DOGGER BANK D — UNITED KINGDOM

THIS POWER PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into
on 14 March 2026 (the "Execution Date"), by and between:

  (1) NORTH SEA WIND HOLDINGS LIMITED, a private limited company
      incorporated under the laws of England and Wales (registered number
      14882016), whose registered office is at 5 Aldermanbury Square,
      London EC2V 7HR ("Seller");
                          and
  (2) BRITISH GREEN UTILITIES PLC, a public limited company
      incorporated under the laws of England and Wales (registered number
      00226600), whose registered office is at 1 Finsbury Avenue,
      London EC2M 2PA ("Buyer").

Buyer and Seller may individually be referred to as a "Party" and
collectively as the "Parties".

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"ACQ" or "Annual Contract Quantity" means an aggregate volume of
3,500,000 MWh of Net Electrical Energy in any Contract Year, subject
to adjustment in accordance with Schedule 4.

"As-Generated Profile" means the half-hourly metered output of the
Facility delivered to the Delivery Point.

"Commercial Operations Date" or "COD" means 1 October 2027.

"Contract Year" means each successive 12-month period starting on the
Commercial Operations Date.

"Delivery Point" means the high-voltage busbar at the National Grid
Electricity Transmission substation at Creyke Beck B (Grid Reference
SE 982 411).

"Facility" means the 1,200 MW offshore wind farm at Dogger Bank Zone
D, comprising up to 92 turbines of 14 MW nameplate capacity each, the
associated array cables, two HVDC offshore platforms and the export
cable to Creyke Beck B.

"GoO" means a Guarantee of Origin issued under the Renewable Energy
Guarantees of Origin (REGO) scheme administered by Ofgem.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 — SALE AND PURCHASE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1  Sale of Energy. Seller shall sell to Buyer, and Buyer shall
purchase from Seller, 100% of the Net Electrical Energy generated by
the Facility on a pay-as-produced basis, up to the ACQ in each
Contract Year, throughout the Term.

2.2  Sale of GoOs. Seller shall transfer to Buyer one (1) GoO for
every megawatt-hour of Net Electrical Energy delivered. GoO transfer
shall occur within ten (10) Business Days of the end of each calendar
month, via the Ofgem REGO registry to a Buyer-nominated account.

2.3  Bundling. The energy and the corresponding GoOs are bundled and
shall be settled together; neither Party shall sell either component
to a third party except as expressly permitted in Article 16.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 — PRICING AND PAYMENT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1  Energy Price. The price payable by Buyer for each MWh of Net
Electrical Energy delivered shall be the greater of:

  (a) the Strike Price of GBP 47.85/MWh (real 2026 prices, indexed
      annually to UK CPI per Schedule 2); or
  (b) the day-ahead N2EX hourly auction settlement price for the
      relevant settlement period.

A floor mechanism of GBP 32.00/MWh applies under negative-pricing
periods (System Length); see Article 7.

3.2  GoO Price. GoOs are priced at GBP 4.50 per certificate during
Years 1–5, escalating to GBP 6.00 per certificate from Year 6 to end
of Term, plus annual UK CPI adjustment.

3.3  Capacity Payment. In addition to the variable energy and GoO
prices, Buyer shall pay a fixed capacity charge of GBP 1,800,000 per
month, payable monthly in arrears, in consideration of Seller
maintaining a guaranteed availability of 95% measured on a rolling
twelve-month basis.

3.4  Invoicing. Seller shall issue an invoice on the fifth (5th)
Business Day of each month showing (a) energy delivered and price,
(b) GoOs transferred and price, and (c) capacity charge. Buyer shall
pay each invoice within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt by
electronic transfer to the account designated in Schedule 5.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 — TERM
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1  Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Execution
Date and shall continue in force for an initial period of fifteen
(15) Contract Years from the Commercial Operations Date (the
"Initial Term").

4.2  Extension Option. Buyer shall have a unilateral right to extend
the Term by an additional five (5) Contract Years on the same
commercial terms by notice given to Seller no later than 24 months
prior to the expiry of the Initial Term.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 — VOLUME COMMITMENT AND CALL OPTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1  Take-or-Pay. Buyer commits to a Take-or-Pay obligation of 80%
of the ACQ in each Contract Year. Any shortfall below 80% shall give
rise to a deficiency payment equal to the Strike Price multiplied by
the volume below the 80% threshold.

5.2  Make-Up Rights. Take-or-Pay deficiency payments paid in any
Contract Year may be applied as credit against MWh purchased in
excess of 100% of ACQ in any of the subsequent three (3) Contract
Years.

5.3  European Call Option on Excess Volume. Buyer holds a European
call option to purchase up to an additional 500,000 MWh per Contract
Year, exercisable on the second-to-last Business Day of each
Contract Year, at a strike price equal to the prevailing N2EX
forward price for delivery in the next Contract Year minus
GBP 5.00/MWh. Premium for this option is GBP 1,250,000 payable
within 30 days of the Commercial Operations Date and annually
thereafter.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 — DELIVERY AND METERING
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1  Delivery. Seller shall deliver Net Electrical Energy at the
Delivery Point on a continuous basis. Title and risk of loss
transfer at the Delivery Point.

6.2  Metering. The Facility's main and check meters shall comply with
Balancing and Settlement Code (BSC) requirements. Seller shall
provide read-only access to half-hourly metering data to Buyer.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 — CURTAILMENT AND COMPENSATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.1  Permitted Curtailment. Seller may curtail output in response to
(a) instructions from the National Electricity System Operator
("NESO"); (b) physical safety constraints; (c) maintenance properly
notified.

7.2  Curtailment Compensation. For any curtailment instructed by
NESO that results in lost generation, Seller shall be entitled to
"deemed energy" payment equal to the Strike Price multiplied by the
volume foregone, capped at 5% of ACQ per Contract Year. Compensation
above the cap is at Seller's risk.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 — CHANGE OF LAW
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.1  Definition. "Change of Law" means any new or amended UK statute,
statutory instrument, regulation, code, license, or binding tribunal
decision adopted after the Execution Date that materially impacts a
Party's rights, obligations or economics under this Agreement,
including any change to the REGO scheme, Contracts for Difference
regime, Renewables Obligation, BSUoS recovery, the UK ETS or carbon
floor price.

8.2  Allocation. The economic burden of any Change of Law shall be
shared 50/50 between the Parties unless the Change of Law specifically
applies to Seller in its capacity as a generator (in which case Seller
bears 100%) or to Buyer in its capacity as a supplier (in which case
Buyer bears 100%). Either Party may invoke this clause within 90 days
of the Change of Law.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 — FORCE MAJEURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.1  Definition. "Force Majeure" means any event or circumstance
beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party including, but
not limited to: acts of God, severe weather (>= once-in-50-year
events), war, terrorism, civil disturbance, fire, explosion, strike
(other than involving the affected Party's own workforce), pandemic
declared by the UK Government, expropriation, and regulatory
prohibition.

9.2  Effect. Performance obligations affected by a Force Majeure
event are suspended for the duration of the event. The Parties shall
use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

9.3  Termination for Prolonged FM. If a Force Majeure event continues
for more than 365 consecutive days, either Party may terminate this
Agreement on 30 days' notice without further liability.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 — TERMINATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

10.1  Termination Events. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
on the occurrence of any of the following:

  (a) failure to pay an undisputed amount within sixty (60) days of
      receipt of written notice of overdue payment;
  (b) any Insolvency Event of the other Party;
  (c) abandonment by Seller of operations of the Facility for more
      than 180 consecutive days;
  (d) prolonged Force Majeure under Article 9.3;
  (e) a final, unappealable order of a UK court declaring this
      Agreement illegal or unenforceable.

10.2  Buyer Discretionary Termination. Buyer may terminate this
Agreement for convenience after the seventh (7th) Contract Year on
twenty-four (24) months' prior written notice, on payment of a
termination fee equal to the discounted present value of two (2)
Contract Years' Capacity Payments (discount rate: UK 10-year gilt
yield + 200 basis points).

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 — PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

11.1  Availability Guarantee. Seller guarantees a rolling-12-month
energy availability of not less than 95% (the "Availability
Threshold"). Availability shall be calculated as actual energy
delivered divided by Theoretical Maximum Energy, excluding only
Permitted Curtailment.

11.2  Liquidated Damages. If average rolling-12-month availability
falls below 95% in any month-end measurement, Seller shall pay
Buyer liquidated damages of GBP 25,000 per percentage point per
month, capped at GBP 2,500,000 in any Contract Year.

11.3  Capacity Factor Floor. If, in any rolling-24-month period,
the realised net capacity factor of the Facility falls below 38%,
Buyer may invoke the price renegotiation provision in Schedule 6.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 12 — INSURANCE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

12.1  Required Coverage. Throughout the Term Seller shall maintain
(a) all-risks property insurance not less than full replacement
value, (b) third-party liability insurance of not less than
GBP 250,000,000 per occurrence, (c) marine insurance for the
offshore equipment, and (d) Directors & Officers insurance.

12.2  Buyer Named Insured. Buyer shall be named as additional
insured on policy (b). Certificates of insurance shall be furnished
to Buyer within 30 days of the Execution Date and on each renewal.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 13 — INDEMNITIES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

13.1  Seller Indemnity. Seller shall indemnify Buyer against any
claim, loss, damage or expense arising from (a) the negligent or
wilful misconduct of Seller or its contractors, (b) breach of any
representation or warranty by Seller, (c) any environmental
contamination caused by the Facility.

13.2  Buyer Indemnity. Buyer shall indemnify Seller against any
claim, loss, damage or expense arising from (a) the negligent or
wilful misconduct of Buyer or its contractors, (b) breach of any
representation or warranty by Buyer.

13.3  Liability Cap. Aggregate liability of either Party arising
under this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to one (1)
Contract Year's Capacity Payments (GBP 21,600,000), excluding
liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 14 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

14.1  Negotiation. The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any
dispute by good-faith negotiation between senior executives within
thirty (30) days of written notice of the dispute.

14.2  Arbitration. If negotiation fails, the dispute shall be
finally resolved by arbitration under the LCIA Rules. The seat
of arbitration shall be London. The number of arbitrators shall
be three (3). The language shall be English.

14.3  Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by and construed
under English law.

SCHEDULE 2 — CPI INDEXATION FORMULA
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

P(year n) = P(year n-1) × (1 + CPI(year n-1)), where CPI(year n-1)
is the average annualised UK CPI rate for year n-1 published by the
Office for National Statistics, capped at +5% and floored at -2%.
