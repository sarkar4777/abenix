THIS CONTRACT FOR DIFFERENCE (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement" or
the "CfD") is entered into on the 22nd day of September, 2024 (the
"Execution Date"),

BETWEEN:

(1) NORTH SEA WIND POWER LTD, a private limited company incorporated and
    registered in England and Wales under company number 14892736, having
    its registered office at One Canada Square, 28th Floor, Canary Wharf,
    London E14 5AB, United Kingdom (hereinafter referred to as the
    "Generator" or the "Seller"); and

(2) NATIONAL GRID ELECTRICITY SYSTEM OPERATOR LIMITED, a company
    incorporated and registered in England and Wales under company number
    11014226, having its registered office at Faraday House, Warwick
    Technology Park, Gallows Hill, Warwick CV34 6DA, United Kingdom, acting
    in its capacity as the Low Carbon Contracts Company ("LCCC") CfD
    Counterparty (hereinafter referred to as the "CfD Counterparty" or
    the "Buyer").

The Generator and the CfD Counterparty are hereinafter collectively referred
to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the United Kingdom Government, through the Department for Energy
Security and Net Zero (DESNZ, formerly BEIS), has established the Contract
for Difference scheme under the Energy Act 2013 to incentivize investment
in low-carbon electricity generation, including offshore wind;

WHEREAS, the Generator was awarded a Contract for Difference in the Fifth
Allocation Round (AR5) conducted by DESNZ for the development, construction,
and operation of a 350 MW offshore wind farm known as Dogger Bank East,
located in the North Sea;

WHEREAS, the Generator has obtained or is in the process of obtaining all
requisite consents, licenses, and approvals for the development,
construction, and operation of the Facility, including a Development Consent
Order (DCO) under the Planning Act 2008;

WHEREAS, the CfD Counterparty is designated under the Energy Act 2013 to
act as the counterparty to Contracts for Difference with low-carbon
electricity generators;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to enter into this Agreement setting out the terms
and conditions under which CfD difference payments will be made in respect
of the electricity generated by the Facility;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations,
warranties, and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1.2 NOTE ON TURBINE CAPACITY

The Siemens Gamesa SG 14-236 DD turbines have a nominal rated capacity of
14 MW per unit. However, the CfD Contracted Capacity for this Facility is
350 MW, which may be achieved through de-rating, contractual capacity limits,
or the installation of 25 units operating at full rated capacity. For the
purposes of this Agreement, the Parties have agreed that the Facility shall
comprise seventy (70) WTGs with a contractual net output cap of 350 MW at
the Offshore Grid Entry Point, with the Generator managing turbine output
accordingly. The total rated capacity of 70 x 14 MW = 980 MW shall be
de-rated to 350 MW at the metering point through agreed operational
protocols.

[ALTERNATIVE INTERPRETATION: If the CfD capacity of 350 MW is to be achieved
with fewer turbines at full rating, then the Facility shall comprise
twenty-five (25) SG 14-236 DD WTGs. The Parties shall agree on the final
turbine count and configuration prior to the Milestone Delivery Date for
Commencement of Offshore Construction.]

For the avoidance of doubt in this contract: the Facility is configured as
25 WTGs x 14 MW each = 350 MW total capacity.

                    ARTICLE 2: TERM AND CONDITIONS PRECEDENT
================================================================================

2.1 TERM

This Agreement shall become effective on the Execution Date and, subject to
the satisfaction of the conditions set out in this Article 2, the CfD
Payment obligations shall commence on the Start Date and shall continue for
a period of fifteen (15) years from the Start Date (the "CfD Term"),
unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of this
Agreement.

2.2 TARGET COMMISSIONING DATE AND LONGSTOP DATE

(a) The Target Commissioning Date is April 1, 2027.

(b) The Longstop Date is October 1, 2028, being eighteen (18) months after
the Target Commissioning Date. If the Generator fails to satisfy the Start
Date conditions on or before the Longstop Date, the CfD Counterparty may
terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the Generator.

(c) The CfD Term shall not be extended by reason of any delay in achieving
the Start Date. The CfD Term is fixed at fifteen (15) years from the actual
Start Date, subject to the Longstop Date.

2.3 MILESTONE REQUIREMENTS

The Generator shall satisfy the following Milestone Requirements by the
Milestone Delivery Dates specified:

(a) Significant Financial Commitment (SFC): The Generator shall demonstrate
that it has committed to spend at least ten percent (10%) of the estimated
total project cost on design, procurement, or construction activities.
Milestone Delivery Date: twelve (12) months after the Execution Date
(September 22, 2025).

(b) Proof of Financing: The Generator shall demonstrate that it has secured
financing (including equity commitments and debt facilities) sufficient to
fund the construction and commissioning of the Facility. Milestone Delivery
Date: March 22, 2026.

(c) Evidence of Consents: The Generator shall provide evidence that it has
obtained all material consents, including the DCO, Marine Licence, and
Electricity Generation Licence. Milestone Delivery Date: twelve (12) months
after the Execution Date (September 22, 2025).

(d) Commencement of Offshore Construction: The Generator shall demonstrate
that offshore construction activities (foundation installation) have
commenced. Milestone Delivery Date: July 1, 2026.

2.4 START DATE CONDITIONS

The Start Date shall occur when the Generator has satisfied all of the
following conditions:

(a) the Facility has been connected to the NETS at the Grid Supply Point
and has been registered in the BSC;

(b) the Generator has obtained all necessary consents, licences, and
approvals for the commercial operation of the Facility;

(c) the Generator has entered into a connection agreement with the relevant
OFTO;

(d) the Settlement Metering Systems have been installed, tested, and
approved by the BSC Central Systems;

(e) the Generator has provided evidence that the Installed Capacity is at
least ninety percent (90%) of the Total Installed Capacity (i.e., at least
315 MW); and

(f) the Generator has delivered to the CfD Counterparty all documents,
certificates, and evidence required under the CfD Standard Terms.

               ARTICLE 3: SALE AND PURCHASE OF ENERGY
================================================================================

3.1 ELECTRICITY SALES

(a) The Generator shall sell all electricity generated by the Facility into
the GB wholesale electricity market through the Balancing Mechanism or
through bilateral power purchase arrangements, in accordance with the
Generator's normal trading activities.

(b) This Agreement does not constitute a contract for the physical sale and
purchase of electricity. The CfD Counterparty is not purchasing electricity
from the Generator. The Agreement governs only the CfD difference payments
between the Strike Price and the Market Reference Price.

3.2 CONTRACTED CAPACITY

The Contracted Capacity under this CfD is 350 MW. The CfD Payments shall
be calculated on the basis of the Actual CfD Generation, subject to the
Maximum Contract Capacity cap of 350 MW.

3.3 EXPECTED ANNUAL GENERATION

Based on the Site's wind resource assessment (P50 net energy yield), the
expected annual generation from the Facility is approximately 1,533 GWh
per year, corresponding to a net capacity factor of approximately 50%.

3.4 NO SHAPE OBLIGATIONS

As an intermittent technology, the Generator has no obligation to deliver
electricity in any particular temporal pattern. The CfD settlement is
based on actual metered output per Settlement Period.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 4: STRIKE PRICE, REFERENCE PRICE, AND SETTLEMENT
================================================================================

4.1 STRIKE PRICE

The Strike Price for the Facility is forty-seven pounds and fifty pence
per megawatt-hour (GBP 47.50/MWh) expressed in 2023 real prices (the
"Base Strike Price").

4.2 MARKET REFERENCE PRICE

The Market Reference Price applicable to each Settlement Period shall be the
GB Day-Ahead Hourly Price for that Settlement Period, as published by the
designated Price Reporting Agency. If no Day-Ahead price is available for a
given Settlement Period, the Market Reference Price shall be determined
using the fallback methodology specified in the CfD Standard Terms.

4.3 CPI INDEXATION

(a) The Administrative Strike Price for each Delivery Year shall be
calculated by adjusting the Base Strike Price for CPI inflation from the
base year (2023) to the relevant Delivery Year, using the following formula:

    Administrative Strike Price(n) = Base Strike Price x (CPI(n) / CPI(base))

Where:
    CPI(n) = the CPI for the twelve-month period ending September in the
             calendar year preceding the start of Delivery Year n;
    CPI(base) = the CPI for the twelve-month period ending September 2023.

(b) The CPI-indexed Strike Price shall be calculated and notified by the CfD
Counterparty to the Generator prior to the start of each Delivery Year.

4.4 CfD SETTLEMENT

(a) For each Settlement Period during the CfD Term:

    (i) If the Administrative Strike Price exceeds the Market Reference Price:
        The CfD Counterparty shall pay to the Generator an amount equal to:
        (Administrative Strike Price - Market Reference Price) x Actual CfD
        Generation for that Settlement Period.

    (ii) If the Market Reference Price exceeds the Administrative Strike Price:
         The Generator shall pay to the CfD Counterparty an amount equal to:
         (Market Reference Price - Administrative Strike Price) x Actual CfD
         Generation for that Settlement Period.

(b) CfD Payments shall be netted and settled on a monthly basis. The CfD
Counterparty shall issue (or the Generator shall issue, as applicable) a
settlement statement within fifteen (15) Business Days after the end of
each calendar month, showing:
    (i) the Actual CfD Generation for each Settlement Period;
    (ii) the Administrative Strike Price;
    (iii) the Market Reference Price for each Settlement Period;
    (iv) the net CfD Payment due; and
    (v) any adjustments for prior months.

(c) Payment of the net CfD Payment shall be due within twenty-eight (28)
days of the settlement statement date.

4.5 NEGATIVE PRICING RULE

(a) No CfD Payment shall be payable by the CfD Counterparty to the
Generator in respect of any Settlement Period during which the Day-Ahead
Hourly Price is negative (i.e., less than GBP 0.00/MWh) (the "Negative
Pricing Rule").

(b) For the avoidance of doubt, if the Generator generates electricity
during a Negative Pricing Settlement Period, the Generator bears all market
price risk for such output, and no CfD top-up payment shall be made.

(c) If six (6) or more consecutive Settlement Periods (three (3) or more
consecutive hours) have negative prices, the Generator is not obligated to
curtail generation, but the Negative Pricing Rule shall apply to all such
Settlement Periods.

4.6 SETTLEMENT DISPUTES

Any dispute regarding settlement calculations, metering data, or payment
amounts shall be raised by the disputing Party within sixty (60) Business
Days of the relevant settlement statement date and shall be resolved in
accordance with the dispute resolution procedures set forth in Article 14.

               ARTICLE 5: METERING AND DATA
================================================================================

5.1 SETTLEMENT METERING SYSTEMS

(a) The Generator shall install, own, and maintain Settlement Metering
Systems at each Settlement Unit in accordance with the BSC and the Grid
Code, comprising:
    (i) revenue-grade energy meters (Class 0.2S accuracy, IEC 62053-22)
        at the Offshore Grid Entry Point;
    (ii) check meters at the onshore Grid Supply Point; and
    (iii) all associated current and voltage transformers, communications
          equipment, and data logging systems.

(b) All Settlement Metering Systems shall be registered with the BSC Central
Systems (administered by Elexon) and shall comply with the CoP
(Code of Practice) applicable to metering at the relevant voltage level.

5.2 WIND DATA

The Generator shall maintain an operational meteorological measurement
system at the Facility, recording wind speed, wind direction, air
temperature, air pressure, humidity, and turbulence intensity at hub height,
with data recorded at ten (10) minute intervals. Such data shall be made
available to the CfD Counterparty upon request for the purposes of verifying
Curtailment claims and Deemed Generation calculations.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 6: PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES
================================================================================

6.1 AVAILABILITY GUARANTEE

(a) The Generator guarantees that the Facility shall achieve an Availability
of not less than ninety-five percent (95%) in each Delivery Year (the
"Guaranteed Availability"), calculated as the time-weighted average of the
availability of all WTGs, excluding:
    (i) periods of Curtailment;
    (ii) periods of Force Majeure;
    (iii) periods of planned maintenance notified to the CfD Counterparty
          at least thirty (30) days in advance; and
    (iv) periods during which the OFTO transmission system is unavailable.

(b) If the Facility's Availability in any Delivery Year falls below the
Guaranteed Availability (after excluding the periods listed in Section
6.1(a)), the CfD Term shall not be extended, but the Generator shall be
required to provide a detailed remediation plan to the CfD Counterparty
within thirty (30) days, setting out the root cause analysis and corrective
actions planned.

6.2 NO MINIMUM GENERATION GUARANTEE

As the Facility is an intermittent generator dependent on the wind resource,
there shall be no minimum generation guarantee. The Generator's obligation
is limited to maintaining the Guaranteed Availability.

6.3 PERFORMANCE REPORTING

The Generator shall submit an Operational Conditions Report to the CfD
Counterparty within sixty (60) days after the end of each Delivery Year,
containing:
    (i) monthly and annual generation data (MWh);
    (ii) Availability data for each WTG and for the Facility as a whole;
    (iii) Curtailment data, including hours and estimated lost generation;
    (iv) significant equipment failures and maintenance activities;
    (v) health, safety, and environmental incident reports; and
    (vi) forward-looking maintenance plans and expected Availability for
         the next twelve (12) months.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 7: CURTAILMENT
================================================================================

7.1 GRID-RELATED CURTAILMENT

(a) If the Generator is instructed by the System Operator (National Grid
ESO) to reduce or cease generation due to transmission constraints,
system balancing requirements, or other grid-related reasons ("Grid
Curtailment"), the Generator shall comply with such instructions promptly.

(b) The Generator shall be entitled to claim compensation for Grid
Curtailment through the Balancing Mechanism (via Bid-Offer Acceptances)
or through the relevant constraint payment mechanisms established by
National Grid ESO.

(c) For the purposes of CfD settlement, the Actual CfD Generation during
periods of Grid Curtailment shall be the actual metered output (which may
be zero or reduced). The CfD Payment shall be calculated based on the
actual metered output only. The Generator's primary recourse for Grid
Curtailment compensation is through the Balancing Mechanism.

7.2 COMPENSATION FOR CURTAILMENT -- CfD DEEMED GENERATION

(a) Notwithstanding Section 7.1(c), if the cumulative Grid Curtailment
in any Delivery Year exceeds five hundred (500) hours, the Generator
may submit a claim to the CfD Counterparty for Deemed Generation CfD
Payments in respect of curtailed hours exceeding the 500-hour threshold.

(b) Deemed Generation for curtailed hours shall be calculated using the
following methodology:
    Deemed Generation (MWh) = Sum over curtailed Settlement Periods of
    [Installed Capacity (MW) x Wind Power Curve Output Factor x
    (Settlement Period Duration in hours)]

    Where Wind Power Curve Output Factor is determined from the
    manufacturer's power curve using the actual hub-height wind speed
    recorded during each curtailed Settlement Period.

(c) The CfD Counterparty shall verify Deemed Generation claims using wind
data provided by the Generator under Article 5.2.

7.3 NEGATIVE PRICING CURTAILMENT

(a) No compensation of any kind (whether through the CfD mechanism or
otherwise from the CfD Counterparty) shall be payable in respect of any
reduction in generation during Settlement Periods with Negative Pricing.

(b) The Generator may choose to curtail or cease generation during Negative
Pricing Settlement Periods at its own discretion. Any such voluntary
curtailment shall not affect the Availability calculation.

================================================================================
               ARTICLE 9: INTERCONNECTION AND GRID CODE
================================================================================

9.1 CONNECTION ARRANGEMENTS

(a) The Generator shall connect the Facility to the NETS at the Grid Supply
Point (Creyke Beck 400 kV) via the offshore and onshore transmission assets.

(b) The offshore transmission assets (from the offshore substation to the
onshore substation boundary) shall be transferred to an Offshore
Transmission Owner (OFTO) under Ofgem's OFTO regime following the
completion of construction.

(c) The Generator shall enter into a Bilateral Embedded Generation Agreement
(BEGA) or other appropriate connection agreement with the relevant
transmission licensee.

9.2 GRID CODE COMPLIANCE

The Facility shall comply with the Grid Code (including the Connection
Conditions and the System Security and Quality of Supply Standards) and
the Distribution Code, including but not limited to:

(a) Frequency Response: The Facility shall provide mandatory frequency
response services, including primary, secondary, and high-frequency
response, in accordance with Grid Code CC.6.3.

(b) Fault Ride-Through: The Facility shall remain connected and stable
during voltage dips to 0% of nominal for up to 140 milliseconds (Phase-to-
Phase fault), and shall support voltage recovery by injecting reactive
current in accordance with Grid Code CC.6.3.15.

(c) Reactive Power Capability: The Facility shall be capable of absorbing
and generating reactive power within a power factor range of 0.95 leading
to 0.95 lagging at the Grid Supply Point, as required by Grid Code CC.6.3.2.

(d) Black Start: The Facility is not required to provide black start
capability unless separately contracted with National Grid ESO.

(e) Communication: The Generator shall maintain a real-time data link
(ICCP/TASE.2 protocol) to the National Grid ESO control centre, providing
telemetry data including active power output, reactive power output, voltage,
frequency, wind speed, and WTG status.

9.3 MARINE LICENCE CONDITIONS

(a) The Generator shall comply with all conditions of the Marine Licence,
including but not limited to:
    (i) pre-construction environmental surveys (benthic ecology, marine
        mammals, ornithology, fish ecology);
    (ii) construction noise mitigation measures (bubble curtains, soft-start
         procedures for piling);
    (iii) in-construction monitoring of underwater noise levels;
    (iv) post-construction monitoring of seabed recovery, marine mammal
         behavior, and bird flight patterns;
    (v) archaeological reporting for any archaeological finds during
        construction;
    (vi) navigational safety measures (aviation and marine lighting,
         marking, and charting);
    (vii) decommissioning plan and financial security.

(b) The Generator shall appoint a Marine Coordinator during the construction
and operational phases to ensure compliance with all marine licence
conditions and to liaise with the Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA),
Trinity House, and the relevant harbour authority.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 10: FORCE MAJEURE
================================================================================

10.1 DEFINITION

A "Force Majeure Event" means any event or circumstance that: (a) is beyond
the reasonable control of the Affected Party; (b) could not reasonably have
been foreseen or prevented by the Affected Party; and (c) directly and
materially prevents or delays the Affected Party from performing its
obligations under this Agreement.

10.2 FORCE MAJEURE EVENTS

Without limitation, Force Majeure Events may include:
(a) storm, cyclone, hurricane, or extreme weather event with wind speeds
    exceeding the WTG design envelope;
(b) tsunami, earthquake, or seismic event;
(c) lightning, flooding, or ice accretion exceeding design parameters;
(d) war, armed conflict, terrorism, sabotage, or civil unrest;
(e) epidemic or pandemic declared by the World Health Organization;
(f) act of government, including compulsory acquisition or nationalization;
(g) general or industry-wide strike or labor action (not specific to the
    Generator or its contractors);
(h) failure or unavailability of the OFTO transmission system not caused by
    the Generator;
(i) port closure or navigational restriction preventing access to the
    offshore Facility for maintenance;
(j) imposition of sanctions or embargoes by the UK Government, EU, or UN.

10.3 EXCLUSIONS

The following shall not constitute Force Majeure Events:
(a) normal variations in wind speed or weather conditions;
(b) equipment failure due to design defects or inadequate maintenance;
(c) financial difficulty, loss of profit, or inability to obtain financing;
(d) failure of a subcontractor or supplier (unless caused by a Force
    Majeure Event);
(e) fluctuations in electricity prices.

10.4 NOTIFICATION AND MITIGATION

The Affected Party shall: (a) notify the other Party within five (5)
Business Days of becoming aware of the Force Majeure Event; (b) use all
reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effects; and (c) provide regular
updates on the status of the Force Majeure Event.

10.5 PROLONGED FORCE MAJEURE

If a Force Majeure Event prevents either Party from performing its material
obligations for a continuous period exceeding twelve (12) months, either
Party may terminate this Agreement by giving ninety (90) days' written
notice.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 11: CHANGE OF LAW
================================================================================

11.1 QUALIFYING CHANGE IN LAW

A "Qualifying Change in Law" means any Change in Law (being the coming into
effect after the Execution Date of any new law, regulation, or binding
directive, or any change in the interpretation or application of existing
law by a Competent Authority) that:

(a) specifically and disproportionately affects the Generator or the
Facility (as compared with other comparable generators in Great Britain);
or

(b) is a Discriminatory Change in Law that targets offshore wind generators
or renewable generators specifically.

11.2 COMPENSATION

If a Qualifying Change in Law occurs that increases the Generator's costs
or reduces the Generator's revenues by more than a de minimis threshold
(currently GBP 500,000 per Delivery Year), the Generator may notify the
CfD Counterparty and the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to agree
an adjustment to the Administrative Strike Price or other commercial terms
to compensate the Generator for the Qualifying Change in Law impact.

11.3 GENERAL CHANGES IN LAW

Changes in law of general application (e.g., changes to corporate tax
rates, general environmental regulations, health and safety requirements)
shall be borne by the Generator and shall not constitute a Qualifying
Change in Law.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 12: TERMINATION
================================================================================

12.1 TERMINATION BY CfD COUNTERPARTY

The CfD Counterparty may terminate this Agreement if:
(a) the Generator fails to satisfy any Milestone Requirement by the
    relevant Milestone Delivery Date;
(b) the Generator fails to achieve the Start Date by the Longstop Date;
(c) the Generator commits a material breach that is not remedied within
    sixty (60) days of written notice;
(d) the Generator becomes insolvent, enters administration, or is subject
    to a winding-up order;
(e) the Generator's Electricity Generation Licence is revoked;
(f) the Generator has made a material misrepresentation in its CfD
    application or in any certificate delivered under this Agreement.

12.2 TERMINATION BY GENERATOR

The Generator may terminate this Agreement if:
(a) the CfD Counterparty fails to make any undisputed CfD Payment within
    ninety (90) days of the due date;
(b) a prolonged Force Majeure Event continues for more than twelve (12)
    months;
(c) a Qualifying Change in Law makes the continued operation of the
    Facility economically unviable, and the Parties have been unable to
    agree on compensating adjustments.

12.3 CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

(a) Upon termination, all future CfD Payment obligations shall cease.

(b) The Generator shall remain liable for any CfD Payments (payable by the
Generator to the CfD Counterparty) in respect of Settlement Periods prior
to the termination date.

(c) The CfD Counterparty shall remain liable for any CfD Payments (payable
by the CfD Counterparty to the Generator) in respect of Settlement Periods
prior to the termination date.

(d) Termination shall not affect any accrued rights or obligations of
either Party.

12.4 TERMINATION PAYMENT

If the CfD Counterparty terminates this Agreement due to a Buyer Event
of Default (failure to pay), the CfD Counterparty shall pay the Generator
a termination payment calculated in accordance with the methodology in
Schedule 6 of the CfD Standard Terms.

and Technology). The expert's determination shall be final and binding
unless either Party refers the matter to arbitration within thirty (30)
days.

14.2 ARBITRATION

Any dispute not resolved by expert determination shall be referred to and
finally resolved by arbitration under the rules of the London Court of
International Arbitration (LCIA), with:
(a) Seat of arbitration: London, England;
(b) Language: English;
(c) Number of arbitrators: Three (3);
(d) Governing law: the laws of England and Wales.

14.3 CONTINUED PERFORMANCE

Pending resolution of any dispute, the Parties shall continue to perform
their respective obligations under this Agreement.
