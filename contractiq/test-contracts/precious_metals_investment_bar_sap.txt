================================================================================
        INVESTMENT BAR SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT
              (Good Delivery 1 kg and 100 g cast bars)

                       CONTRACT NO. IBP-2026-061

                  DATE OF EXECUTION: 5 March 2026
================================================================================

BETWEEN:

(1) HELVETIA METALS REFINING AG, a Swiss stock corporation registered in
    the Commercial Register of the Canton of Zurich under CHE-104.582.913
    (the "Seller");

(2) MERIDIAN BULLION DCC LTD, a private limited company organised under
    the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre, with its
    registered office at Office 1701, Index Tower, DIFC, Dubai, United Arab
    Emirates (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS the Seller is a refiner on the LBMA Good Delivery list for Gold and
the LBMA Good Delivery list for Silver, and the Buyer is a wholesale bullion
distributor; the parties wish to record the terms on which the Seller shall
sell investment-grade gold bars to the Buyer for the calendar year 2026 to
2027.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Product. The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, cast
    gold investment bars in the following weights and minimum fineness:

      (a) 1 kg cast bars, fineness 999.9 (four-nines fine);
      (b) 100 g cast bars, fineness 999.9.

1.2 Bar specifications. Each bar shall comply with LBMA Good Delivery Rules
    for Gold version 1.1 of August 2023, with the following clarifications:

      (a) every bar shall carry the Seller's refinery hallmark, a unique
          serial number, the year of casting, the gross weight to four
          decimal places of a troy ounce, and the fineness expressed as
          parts per thousand;

      (b) the cast surface shall be free of porosity, cracks, scratches
          deeper than 0.1 mm, slag inclusions, or any visible alteration
          to the hallmark.

1.3 Brand. All bars shall be of the Seller's "Helvetia Refined" branded
    series. The Buyer represents that the Seller's branded bars are
    accepted on the same terms as bars from the following refiners on the
    Buyer's accepted-refiners list: Metalor Technologies SA, Heraeus
    Precious Metals GmbH, Tanaka Kikinzoku Kogyo K.K., Valcambi SA,
    Argor-Heraeus SA, Rand Refinery (Pty) Ltd, Asahi Refining USA Inc, and
    The Royal Canadian Mint.

1.4 Volumes. The Seller shall offer, and the Buyer shall purchase, a
    minimum of forty thousand (40,000) troy ounces of fine gold in 1 kg
    bars per calendar quarter and a minimum of fifteen thousand (15,000)
    troy ounces of fine gold in 100 g bars per calendar quarter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PRICING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Reference price. The price of each consignment shall be:

       Price per fine troy ounce = (LBMA Gold PM Fix on the Pricing Date)
                                   + Loco Zurich premium of 0.75%
                                   + Manufacturing premium of USD 0.85 per troy ounce

2.2 Pricing Date. The Buyer shall fix the price for each consignment on or
    before the Seller's notified Pricing Window. The Pricing Window is the
    two business days commencing on the date of shipment notification.

2.3 Settlement currency. All payments shall be made in United States
    Dollars (USD) by SWIFT MT-103 to the Seller's nominated account.

2.4 Payment terms. Payment shall be made on T+2 (two business days after
    the Pricing Date) by irrevocable wire transfer. The bars shall be
    released from the Seller's vault to the Buyer's nominated logistics
    agent on confirmation of cleared funds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LOCO AND DELIVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Loco. All sales shall be loco Zurich. Title and risk pass at the
    moment the bars are released to the Buyer's nominated logistics agent
    at the Seller's vault gate in Mendrisio, Switzerland.

3.2 Insurance. Up to the point of release, the Seller shall maintain
    all-risk insurance at one hundred and ten per cent (110%) of bar
    value. After release, the Buyer is solely responsible for insurance.

3.3 Repatriation. The Buyer may, on at least seven (7) business days'
    notice, request that bars instead be delivered loco London or loco
    Dubai. The Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to arrange
    onward transport at the Buyer's cost; risk in transit shall remain
    with the Buyer.

3.4 Chain of integrity. Each bar shall be tracked in the Seller's vault
    management system from production to gate-out. The Seller shall
    provide a serialised manifest of every consignment to the Buyer at
    least twenty-four (24) hours prior to release.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ASSAY AND REJECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 The Buyer may, at the Buyer's expense, conduct random destructive
    assay on up to one per cent (1%) of bars per consignment by quantity,
    using fire assay at any LBMA-recognised laboratory.

4.2 Tolerance. Where the Buyer's assay reveals a fineness below 999.7 on
    any bar, the entire batch from which the bar was sampled shall be
    rejected and replaced by the Seller at the Seller's cost.

4.3 Umpire. Where the Buyer and the Seller's assays differ by more than
    two-hundredths of one per cent (0.02%), the parties shall instruct
    an umpire assay; the loser bears the umpire's costs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMPLIANCE AND SANCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 LBMA. The Seller represents and warrants that all bars produced
    hereunder originate from material that has passed the Seller's LBMA
    Responsible Gold Guidance v9 due diligence process.

5.2 OECD. The Seller maintains a documented OECD Due Diligence programme
    aligned with the OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply
    Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas, 3rd
    edition (2016).

5.3 Excluded origins. The Seller represents that no material delivered
    hereunder originates from, or has been refined in, the Russian
    Federation, the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, the Islamic
    Republic of Iran, or any other jurisdiction subject to comprehensive
    sanctions imposed by OFAC, the European Union, or the United Nations
    at the time of delivery.

5.4 OFAC. Each party warrants that it is not, and is not owned or
    controlled by, a person on the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals
    list, the EU Consolidated List, the UK Sanctions List, or the
    Swiss SECO sanctions list.

5.5 VAT. The parties acknowledge that 1 kg and 100 g bars of 999.9
    fineness produced and sold hereunder qualify as "investment gold"
    within the meaning of HMRC Notice 701/14 (Investment Gold Coins) and
    are exempt from United Kingdom VAT.

5.6 PMCA. The Seller shall complete every filing required by the Swiss
    Precious Metals Control Act.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TERM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 This Agreement shall be effective from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027,
    renewing automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless
    either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal.

6.2 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if:
    (a) the other ceases to hold a current LBMA Good Delivery listing;
    (b) the other becomes the subject of a sanctions action;
    (c) the other becomes insolvent.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. GENERAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Governing law and forum. Swiss law; arbitration in Zurich under the
    Swiss Rules of International Arbitration, by a single arbitrator.

7.2 Confidentiality applies to all assay reports, serial-number lists and
    chain-of-custody manifests.
