LNG TOLLING AND PROCESSING AGREEMENT
(Qatargas 4 — Ras Laffan Train 7)

BETWEEN
(1) QATAR LIQUEFIED GAS COMPANY LIMITED (4) — "Processor"
    a joint stock company organised under the laws of the State of Qatar, whose principal office is at West Bay, Doha, Qatar.
(2) GLOBAL ENERGY TRADING EUROPE B.V. — "Offtaker"
    a private company organised under the laws of the Netherlands, whose principal office is at Weena 722, 3014 DA Rotterdam.

EXECUTION DATE: 14 May 2025
COMMENCEMENT DATE: 1 January 2027
TERM: Twenty (20) Years + two optional 5-year extensions
REFERENCE: QG4-TOL-2025-0074

BACKGROUND
(A) The Processor operates LNG Train 7 at Ras Laffan Industrial City with a nameplate liquefaction capacity of 7.8 MTPA.
(B) The Offtaker wishes to secure long-term tolling rights for 2.4 MTPA of the Train's output, delivered Ex-Ship or FOB Ras Laffan.
(C) The Parties have agreed the commercial, operational and financial terms set out in this Agreement.

ARTICLE 1  DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Annual Contract Quantity" (ACQ) means 2,400,000 metric tonnes of LNG per Contract Year.
1.2 "Delivered Quantity" means LNG delivered FOB Ras Laffan or DES at a Mutually Agreed Regasification Terminal.
1.3 "Reference Price" means the Japan Korea Marker (JKM) month-ahead published price in USD/MMBtu.
1.4 "Tolling Fee" means the fixed fee per MMBtu processed, indexed annually to US CPI per Article 6.2.
1.5 "Take-or-Pay Obligation" means the Offtaker's obligation under Article 7.
1.6 "Make-Up LNG" means LNG the Offtaker is entitled to lift in subsequent years per Article 7.3.
1.7 "Dayrate" means the vessel hire rate applicable to Processor-provided LNG carriers at USD 95,000/day (indexed).
1.8 "Available Capacity" has the meaning in Article 3.2.
1.9 "Force Majeure" has the meaning in Article 17.
1.10 "Quality Specification" means the gas quality parameters in Schedule 3 (HHV 1,055-1,120 BTU/scf, Wobbe Index 47-52, max H2S 3.5 ppm).

ARTICLE 2  SCOPE OF TOLLING SERVICES
2.1 The Processor shall, during each Contract Year, process the Offtaker's pipeline gas feedstock (sourced from North Field production via Dolphin pipeline) into LNG and make available 2.4 MTPA ACQ for lifting by the Offtaker.
2.2 Feedstock supply is provided by Qatar Petroleum under a separate Upstream Supply Agreement; the Offtaker shall not have direct upstream supply risk.
2.3 Processing includes: feed gas receipt, acid gas removal, dehydration, mercury removal, NGL recovery, liquefaction, storage (2 × 160,000 m³ tanks), and loading.

ARTICLE 3  CAPACITY RIGHTS
3.1 The Offtaker has firm tolling rights to 2.4 MTPA, being approximately 30.8% of Train 7's nameplate capacity.
3.2 Available Capacity is the nameplate capacity less scheduled maintenance (30 days/year) less force majeure outages.
3.3 The Processor shall give 90 days' advance notice of any scheduled maintenance window exceeding 5 days.
3.4 Capacity reallocation during unplanned outages shall be pro-rata among all Train 7 offtakers.

ARTICLE 4  DELIVERY SCHEDULE AND NOMINATION
4.1 The Offtaker shall submit an Annual Delivery Program (ADP) by 1 October for the following Contract Year, specifying target monthly liftings.
4.2 Monthly nominations shall be confirmed by the 20th day of the prior month; changes thereafter require Processor agreement and may incur rescheduling fees of USD 250,000 per cargo.
4.3 Minimum cargo size: 135,000 m³ (approx. 58,000 tonnes). Maximum: 180,000 m³.
4.4 Each cargo shall be loaded within a 72-hour laycan agreed at least 15 days in advance.

ARTICLE 5  VESSELS AND SHIPPING
5.1 The Offtaker shall provide or arrange LNG vessels meeting OCIMF SIRE standards, with Q-Flex or Q-Max equivalent tonnage for the Far East route.
5.2 Demurrage rate: USD 115,000/day plus VAT, prorated for part-days, payable within 30 days of invoice.
5.3 Free loading time: 36 hours from NOR tendered. Processor-caused delay beyond 48 hours triggers liquidated damages of USD 55,000/day.
5.4 Title and risk pass FOB flange on vessel manifold connection for DES cargoes title transfers at discharge flange.

ARTICLE 6  TOLLING FEE AND INDEXATION
6.1 Base Tolling Fee: USD 2.75/MMBtu (2024 real) applicable from the Commencement Date.
6.2 Indexation: Annual adjustment on 1 January equal to the prior calendar year change in US CPI-U (Bureau of Labor Statistics series CUUR0000SA0). Cap of +4%, floor of -1% per year.
6.3 Processing Cost Pass-Through: Electricity, catalysts, amines, maintenance — charged at cost + 8% management fee, reconciled quarterly.
6.4 Fuel Gas: The Processor retains up to 6.5% of feed gas as fuel; any excess is billed at internal transfer pricing equivalent to JKM minus USD 2.
6.5 Environmental Levy: USD 0.15/MMBtu per the Qatari CO2 emission fee, indexed to the Qatar carbon price index.

ARTICLE 7  TAKE-OR-PAY AND MAKE-UP
7.1 Annual Minimum Bill (AMB) = 85% of ACQ × Tolling Fee.
7.2 If the Offtaker lifts less than 85% of ACQ in a Contract Year, it shall pay the Tolling Fee on the shortfall ("T-o-P Payment").
7.3 T-o-P Payments create Make-Up Rights — the Offtaker may lift make-up LNG over the following 5 Contract Years without paying an additional Tolling Fee, subject to Available Capacity.
7.4 Unused Make-Up Rights expire after 60 months and are forfeited (no cash refund).
7.5 Carry-forward: The Offtaker may lift up to 110% of ACQ in any year by consuming Available Capacity.

ARTICLE 8  PRICING FOR DES / RESALE CARGOES
8.1 Where the Offtaker elects DES delivery (Processor-arranged shipping), the cargo price = JKM month-ahead × (1 - 0.03) + Tolling Fee + Shipping Pass-Through.
8.2 Processor's shipping pass-through includes charter hire, boil-off (0.12%/day), port dues, canal fees.
8.3 The Offtaker may request monthly volume rebalancing between FOB and DES up to ±15% of nominated volume.

ARTICLE 9  PAYMENT
9.1 Invoices issued within 5 Business Days of Bill of Lading. Due 20 days from invoice date.
9.2 Currency: USD. Bank: JPMorgan Chase, New York.
9.3 Late payment interest: SOFR + 300 bps from due date, compounded monthly.
9.4 Disputed invoices — the undisputed portion is payable when due; disputed amounts are escrowed.

ARTICLE 10  CREDIT SUPPORT
10.1 The Offtaker shall at all times maintain credit support acceptable to the Processor:
     (a) Investment-grade Parent Company Guarantee (minimum Moody's Baa2 / S&P BBB), OR
     (b) Standby Letter of Credit from Tier-1 bank equal to 90 days' AMB, OR
     (c) Cash deposit equal to 60 days' AMB.
10.2 Credit support threshold: Mark-to-Market exposure reviewed monthly. Exposure in excess of USD 30M triggers additional margin at 110% cover.
10.3 Rating downgrade below Baa2/BBB triggers 15-Business-Day cure period to provide enhanced credit support.

ARTICLE 11  METERING AND QUANTITY DETERMINATION
11.1 Quantity measured by vessel tank gauging per ISO 10976 using Processor's onshore custody transfer meters (ultrasonic, 0.25% accuracy).
11.2 Energy content calculated from GC analysis per GIIGNL standard, reported in MMBtu HHV.
11.3 Independent surveyor: SGS or Intertek, appointed by Offtaker, cost shared 50/50.
11.4 Measurement disputes — recalculation per ISO 6976 within 30 days; binding decision by Saybolt after.

ARTICLE 12  QUALITY
12.1 Delivered LNG shall meet Schedule 3 specifications.
12.2 Off-spec cargo: the Offtaker may reject or accept with Processor paying reprocessing cost + USD 500k quality allowance.
12.3 Consistent quality deviation (3 consecutive cargoes) triggers Processor-funded root-cause analysis.

ARTICLE 13  ASSIGNMENT AND DESTINATION FLEXIBILITY
13.1 The Offtaker has full destination flexibility for FOB cargoes — no destination restrictions except US sanctioned countries.
13.2 The Offtaker may nominate its Affiliates as lifters without Processor consent provided the Affiliate meets credit support requirements.
13.3 Third-party assignment requires Processor consent (not to be unreasonably withheld) and assignee credit due diligence.

ARTICLE 14  REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
14.1 Both parties shall comply with all applicable export controls (US OFAC, EU sanctions, UK OFSI).
14.2 Restricted destinations: DPRK, Iran, Syria, Cuba, and any entity on the US SDN list.
14.3 The Offtaker warrants end-use is for legitimate regasification and distribution; no diversion for blending with other-origin LNG that contravenes sanctions.

ARTICLE 15  DECLARATION OF NET-ZERO AND GHG REPORTING
15.1 The Processor provides a lifecycle GHG intensity certificate for each cargo per GIIGNL Methodological Framework v2.
15.2 The Offtaker is entitled to monetise methane emissions certificates from the supply chain ("MiQ-certified cargoes").
15.3 From 2030, Scope 1+2 emissions per cargo shall not exceed 0.35 tCO2e/tLNG delivered (rolling 12-month average).

ARTICLE 16  CHANGE OF LAW / CHANGE OF ECONOMIC CIRCUMSTANCES
16.1 Material Change of Law — either party may request re-opener if legislation (Qatari, EU, UK, US) materially affects the economics beyond 10% of annual value.
16.2 Good-faith re-opener window: 90 days of documented negotiation, escalation to CEO-level, then arbitration.

ARTICLE 17  FORCE MAJEURE
17.1 Force Majeure events: acts of God, war, terrorism, strikes, government acts, embargoes, pipeline/plant outages beyond Processor's control, Suez / Panama / Hormuz chokepoint closures, and other comparable events beyond reasonable control.
17.2 Notification deadline: 48 hours from knowledge of event, with detailed particulars within 10 days.
17.3 Suspension: affected obligations suspended during continuance; ACQ adjusted proportionally.
17.4 Termination right: if FM continues beyond 180 continuous days, either party may terminate without further liability.

ARTICLE 18  TERMINATION
18.1 Events of default:
     (a) Failure to pay undisputed sums 30 days after due date.
     (b) Insolvency, administration, or composition with creditors.
     (c) Material breach of credit support obligations under Article 10.
     (d) Breach of sanctions obligations under Article 14.
18.2 Cure period: 30 Business Days for (a); no cure for (b) or (d).
18.3 Termination Payment: the defaulting party pays the non-defaulting party a close-out amount = PV of net-losses over remaining term, discounted at JKM forward curve.

ARTICLE 19  LIABILITY AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
19.1 Aggregate cap: USD 2 billion per annum.
19.2 Consequential, indirect, and lost profits excluded except for Article 7 and Article 18 amounts.
19.3 LNG shortfall LDs: USD 8/MMBtu on unliquefied volumes below 90% of ACQ, payable monthly.

ARTICLE 20  GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
20.1 This Agreement is governed by English law, excluding its conflict of laws rules.
20.2 Dispute resolution: ICC arbitration, 3-arbitrator panel, seat in London, language English, emergency arbitrator per 2021 ICC Rules.
20.3 Confidentiality applies to all arbitration proceedings.

ARTICLE 21  CONFIDENTIALITY
21.1 Commercial terms are confidential for 10 years post-termination.
21.2 Disclosure permitted to: advisors (under NDA), regulators, investors in M&A process (under NDA).
