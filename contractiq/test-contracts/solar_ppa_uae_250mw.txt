entered into on the 15th day of March, 2024 (the "Execution Date"),

BETWEEN:

(1) AL DHAFRA SOLAR ENERGY LLC, a limited liability company incorporated and
    existing under the laws of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates,
    having its registered office at Plot C-42, Al Dhafra Industrial Zone,
    Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, P.O. Box 94837, and registered with the
    Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development under Commercial License
    No. CN-3489271 (hereinafter referred to as the "Seller"); and

(2) ABU DHABI DISTRIBUTION COMPANY (ADDC), a public joint stock company
    incorporated and existing under the laws of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi,
    United Arab Emirates, having its registered office at Khalifa Business
    Park, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, P.O. Box 219, and regulated by
    the Department of Energy (DoE) of Abu Dhabi under License
    No. DoE/DL/2023/0084 (hereinafter referred to as the "Buyer").

The Seller and the Buyer are hereinafter collectively referred to as the
"Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Government of Abu Dhabi, through the Abu Dhabi Department of
Energy ("DoE"), has established a regulatory framework to promote the
development of renewable energy generation capacity within the Emirate of
Abu Dhabi, consistent with the UAE Energy Strategy 2050 and Abu Dhabi's
commitment to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2050;

WHEREAS, the Seller has been selected through a competitive international
tender process conducted by the Emirates Water and Electricity Company
(EWEC) and the Abu Dhabi Department of Energy to develop, finance, construct,
own, and operate a 250 MW (AC) solar photovoltaic power generation facility
(the "Facility") located in the Al Dhafra region of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi;

WHEREAS, the Seller has secured all necessary development rights, land use
agreements, and preliminary approvals from the relevant governmental
authorities for the development and operation of the Facility;

WHEREAS, the Buyer is a licensed electricity distribution company in the
Emirate of Abu Dhabi, authorized to purchase electricity from independent
power producers and distribute the same to end consumers within its licensed
distribution area;

WHEREAS, the Buyer desires to purchase, and the Seller desires to sell, the
entire net electrical output of the Facility on the terms and conditions set
forth in this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations,
warranties, and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable
consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged,
the Parties agree as follows:

                    ARTICLE 2: TERM AND CONDITIONS PRECEDENT
================================================================================

2.1 TERM

This Agreement shall become effective on the Effective Date and, unless
earlier terminated in accordance with its terms, shall remain in full force
and effect for a period of twenty-five (25) years from the Commercial
Operation Date (the "Term"). Notwithstanding the foregoing, the obligations
of the Parties set forth in Articles 1 through 5 of this Agreement (to the
extent applicable to the pre-COD period), and all other provisions that by
their nature are intended to apply prior to the Commercial Operation Date,
shall become effective on the Effective Date.

2.2 LONG-STOP DATE

(a) The Facility shall achieve Commercial Operation no later than
December 31, 2026 (the "Long-Stop Date"). If the Facility fails to achieve
Commercial Operation by the Long-Stop Date, except to the extent caused by
Force Majeure, Buyer Default, or Change of Law, the Buyer shall have the
right to terminate this Agreement by giving not less than thirty (30)
Business Days' written notice to the Seller.

(b) The Long-Stop Date shall be extended day-for-day for any delay caused
by: (i) Force Majeure; (ii) Buyer Default; (iii) Change of Law;
(iv) failure by the Grid Operator to complete its interconnection obligations
within the timeframe specified in Article 9; or (v) any other event or
circumstance expressly stated in this Agreement to entitle the Seller to an
extension of the Long-Stop Date; provided, however, that the aggregate
extension of the Long-Stop Date pursuant to this Section 2.2(b) shall not
exceed twenty-four (24) months.

2.3 CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement (other than those that
are expressly stated to take effect on the Execution Date or the Effective
Date) shall be subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following
conditions precedent (the "Conditions Precedent") on or before the Closing
Date:

(a) Governmental Approvals: The Seller shall have obtained all material
governmental approvals, permits, licenses, and authorizations required for
the development, construction, and operation of the Facility, including
without limitation:
    (i) the generation license from the Department of Energy of Abu Dhabi;
    (ii) the environmental clearance from the Environment Agency Abu Dhabi (EAD);
    (iii) the building permits from the Abu Dhabi Municipality;
    (iv) the water and wastewater discharge permits;
    (v) the aviation safety clearance from the General Civil Aviation Authority; and
    (vi) any other permits required under Applicable Law.

(b) Land Rights: The Seller shall have executed a land lease agreement or
usufruct right with the relevant Governmental Authority for the Site,
granting the Seller exclusive use and possession of the Site for a period
not less than the Term plus five (5) years.

(c) Interconnection Agreement: The Seller and the Grid Operator shall have
executed a grid connection agreement on terms reasonably acceptable to the
Buyer, providing for the interconnection of the Facility to the Grid at the
Delivery Point.

(d) Insurance: The Seller shall have obtained and delivered to the Buyer
evidence of all insurance policies required under Article 10, in form and
substance satisfactory to the Buyer.

(e) Credit Support: The Seller shall have delivered to the Buyer the Letter
of Credit and the Parent Company Guarantee in accordance with Article 14.

(f) Financing: The Seller shall have achieved Financial Close with respect
to the financing of the Facility.

(g) EPC Contract: The Seller shall have executed an engineering, procurement,
and construction ("EPC") contract for the Facility with a reputable EPC
contractor.

(h) O&M Arrangements: The Seller shall have entered into, or shall have
demonstrated it has the capability to perform, all necessary operation and
maintenance arrangements for the Facility.

2.4 WAIVER OF CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

Any Condition Precedent may be waived in whole or in part by written
agreement of the Parties. Such waiver shall not prejudice any other right or
remedy of the waiving Party under this Agreement.

2.5 COMMERCIAL OPERATION DATE

The Facility shall be deemed to have achieved Commercial Operation when the
following conditions have been satisfied:

(a) The Facility has been constructed and commissioned in accordance with
the Technical Specifications set forth in Schedule 1;

(b) The Facility has successfully completed all Testing required under
Schedule 1, as certified by the Independent Engineer;

(c) The Revenue Meters have been installed, tested, and calibrated in
accordance with Article 5;

(d) All interconnection facilities have been completed and energized, and
the Facility is connected to the Grid at the Delivery Point;

(e) The Seller has obtained all governmental approvals, permits, and licenses
required for the commercial operation of the Facility;

(f) The Independent Engineer has issued a certificate confirming that the
Facility has achieved a Capacity of not less than ninety-five percent (95%)
of the Contracted Capacity; and

(g) The Facility has operated continuously for a period of not less than
seventy-two (72) hours at a minimum of eighty percent (80%) of the
Contracted Capacity during daylight hours.

                 ARTICLE 3: SALE AND PURCHASE OF ENERGY
================================================================================

3.1 OBLIGATION TO SELL AND PURCHASE

(a) Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Seller shall
sell and deliver, and the Buyer shall purchase and accept, all Net Electrical
Output generated by the Facility and delivered to the Delivery Point during
the Term.

(b) The Buyer shall be obligated to purchase one hundred percent (100%) of
the Net Electrical Output delivered by the Seller to the Delivery Point,
subject only to Curtailment in accordance with Article 7 and Force Majeure
events.

3.2 CONTRACTED CAPACITY

The Contracted Capacity of the Facility shall be two hundred and fifty
megawatts (250 MW AC). The Seller shall not increase the Contracted Capacity
of the Facility without the prior written consent of the Buyer.

3.3 MINIMUM ANNUAL DELIVERY

(a) The Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to generate and
deliver to the Delivery Point in each Contract Year a Net Electrical Output
of not less than four hundred and seventy-five gigawatt-hours (475 GWh) (the
"Minimum Annual Delivery"), subject to adjustment for Degradation as provided
in Schedule 1.

(b) For the avoidance of doubt, the Minimum Annual Delivery shall be reduced
proportionally for any partial Contract Year and shall be adjusted downward
for: (i) periods of Compensable Curtailment; (ii) Force Majeure events;
(iii) Scheduled Maintenance approved by the Buyer; and (iv) any other
events expressly excused under this Agreement.

3.4 SHAPE OF DELIVERY

The Seller acknowledges that the Facility's energy output will be
intermittent and variable in nature, dependent upon the Solar Resource at
the Site. The Buyer shall accept the Net Electrical Output in whatever
temporal pattern it is naturally produced by the Facility, and there shall
be no obligation on the Seller to deliver energy in any particular hourly,
daily, weekly, or seasonal profile or shape.

3.5 EXCLUSIVITY

During the Term, the Seller shall not sell, deliver, or commit to sell or
deliver the Net Electrical Output or any portion thereof to any Person other
than the Buyer, except with the prior written consent of the Buyer or as
otherwise expressly permitted under this Agreement.

               ARTICLE 4: TARIFF AND PAYMENT
================================================================================

4.1 BASE TARIFF

(a) The Buyer shall pay to the Seller, for each MWh of Net Electrical Output
delivered at the Delivery Point and recorded by the Revenue Meters, the Base
Tariff, which for Contract Years 1 through 5 shall be equal to seventeen
United States Dollars and eighty-six cents per megawatt-hour (USD 17.86/MWh),
equivalent to one point seven eight six United States cents per kilowatt-hour
(USD 0.01786/kWh).

(b) This Base Tariff represents the levelized cost-of-energy bid submitted
by the Seller and accepted by the Buyer in the competitive tender process
and reflects all costs of developing, financing, constructing, owning,
operating, maintaining, and decommissioning the Facility over the Term.

4.2 TARIFF ESCALATION

(a) Commencing from Contract Year 6 and for each Contract Year thereafter
during the Term, the Base Tariff shall be escalated annually at a fixed rate
of two percent (2%) per annum, compounded annually (the "Escalation Rate").

(b) The escalated tariff for any Contract Year n (where n > 5) shall be
calculated as follows:

    Tariff(n) = Base Tariff x (1 + 0.02)^(n-5)

(c) The year-by-year tariff schedule is set forth in Schedule 4 attached
hereto.

4.3 INVOICING

(a) Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each Billing Period, the
Seller shall submit to the Buyer a monthly invoice (each, an "Invoice")
specifying:
    (i) the Billing Period to which the Invoice relates;
    (ii) the total Net Electrical Output delivered to the Delivery Point
         during the Billing Period, as recorded by the Revenue Meters;
    (iii) the applicable tariff rate for the relevant Contract Year;
    (iv) the total amount payable by the Buyer for such Billing Period;
    (v) any adjustments for Deemed Generation, Curtailment Energy, or
         metering corrections;
    (vi) any amounts previously disputed and subsequently resolved;
    (vii) any Liquidated Damages payable or creditable; and
    (viii) the net amount payable.

(b) Each Invoice shall be accompanied by:
    (i) Revenue Meter data in electronic format;
    (ii) meteorological data (irradiance, ambient temperature, wind speed)
         from the Facility's weather stations;
    (iii) availability reports;
    (iv) Curtailment reports, if applicable; and
    (v) such other supporting documentation as reasonably requested by the
         Buyer.

4.4 PAYMENT

(a) The Buyer shall pay each Invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt
of a complete and properly rendered Invoice (the "Payment Due Date").
Payments shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds in
United States Dollars to the Seller's designated bank account as notified
by the Seller in writing.

(b) If the Buyer disputes any amount in an Invoice in good faith, the Buyer
shall pay the undisputed portion of the Invoice on or before the Payment Due
Date and shall notify the Seller in writing of the disputed amount and the
basis for the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days of receipt of the
Invoice.

(c) Any disputed amounts that are subsequently resolved in favor of the
Seller shall bear interest at the Default Interest Rate from the original
Payment Due Date until the date of actual payment.

4.5 LATE PAYMENT INTEREST

If the Buyer fails to pay any undisputed amount on or before the Payment
Due Date, the unpaid amount shall bear interest from the Payment Due Date
until the date of actual payment at the Default Interest Rate (SOFR plus
two percent (2%) per annum), calculated on a daily compounding basis. Late
payment interest shall be payable in addition to, and not in substitution
for, any other right or remedy of the Seller under this Agreement or at
law.

4.6 TAXES

(a) The Base Tariff is exclusive of any value added tax ("VAT") or similar
indirect tax that may be imposed by any Governmental Authority on the sale
and purchase of electrical energy. Any such VAT or similar tax shall be added
to the Invoice and paid by the Buyer.

(b) Each Party shall be responsible for its own income taxes, corporate
taxes, and similar direct taxes arising from or in connection with this
Agreement.

(c) Any withholding taxes required to be deducted from payments under this
Agreement shall be borne by the paying Party unless otherwise required by
Applicable Law, in which case the paying Party shall gross up the payment
such that the receiving Party receives the full amount that would have been
received absent such withholding.


================================================================================
               ARTICLE 5: METERING AND SETTLEMENT
================================================================================

5.1 REVENUE METERS

(a) The Seller shall install, own, and maintain a primary set of revenue-
grade energy meters (the "Revenue Meters") at the Delivery Point for the
purpose of measuring the Net Electrical Output delivered by the Facility
to the Buyer's network.

(b) The Revenue Meters shall comply with IEC 62053-22 (Class 0.2S accuracy)
or equivalent standard, and shall measure active energy (kWh), reactive
energy (kVArh), power factor, voltage, and current at fifteen (15) minute
intervals.

(c) The Buyer shall have the right, at its own cost, to install a set of
check meters (the "Check Meters") at or near the Delivery Point. The Check
Meters shall be used for verification purposes only and shall not be used
for billing unless the Revenue Meters are found to be inaccurate.

5.2 METER READING AND DATA

(a) The Revenue Meters shall be equipped with automated data logging and
remote communication capabilities, allowing both Parties to access real-time
and historical metering data.

(b) The Seller shall provide the Buyer with electronic access to all Revenue
Meter data on a continuous, real-time basis through a secure data link.

(c) Monthly meter readings shall be taken on the last day of each Billing
Period (or the next Business Day if the last day falls on a non-Business Day)
and shall be used as the basis for invoicing.

5.3 METER TESTING AND CALIBRATION

(a) The Revenue Meters shall be tested and calibrated at least once every
twelve (12) months, or more frequently if requested by either Party, by an
independent, accredited testing laboratory at the Seller's cost.

(b) If any test reveals that a Revenue Meter has an error exceeding plus or
minus 0.5%, the Seller shall promptly recalibrate or replace the meter at
its own cost, and the Parties shall agree on an adjustment to the metered
data for the period from the last satisfactory test to the date of
correction.

(c) If the Revenue Meters fail or cease to register, the Parties shall use
the Check Meter data, and in the absence of Check Meter data, shall estimate
the Net Electrical Output based on the Facility's generation profile during
comparable periods, meteorological data, and other relevant factors.

5.4 METER DISPUTES

Any dispute regarding metering data, meter accuracy, or adjustments to
metered data shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution

               ARTICLE 6: PERFORMANCE GUARANTEES
================================================================================

6.1 AVAILABILITY GUARANTEE

(a) The Seller guarantees that the Facility shall achieve an Availability of
not less than ninety-eight percent (98%) in each Contract Year (the
"Guaranteed Availability").

(b) If the Facility's Availability in any Contract Year falls below the
Guaranteed Availability, the Seller shall pay to the Buyer Liquidated
Damages in an amount equal to: (i) the product of (A) the shortfall
in Availability (expressed as a percentage) multiplied by (B) the Annual
Contract Quantity for such Contract Year multiplied by (C) the applicable
Base Tariff for such Contract Year.

(c) For the avoidance of doubt, Excused Hours shall be excluded from the
calculation of Availability.

6.2 PERFORMANCE RATIO GUARANTEE

(a) The Seller guarantees that the Performance Ratio of the Facility, as
measured and calculated in accordance with IEC 61724, shall be not less than
eighty percent (80%) over each Contract Year (the "Performance Ratio
Guarantee"), after adjustment for Degradation.

(b) If the Facility's Performance Ratio in any Contract Year falls below the
Performance Ratio Guarantee, the Seller shall pay to the Buyer Liquidated
Damages equal to: (i) fifty United States Dollars per megawatt-hour
(USD 50/MWh) multiplied by the shortfall in energy output (in MWh)
attributable to the Performance Ratio deficiency, where such shortfall
shall be calculated as the difference between the actual energy output and
the energy output that would have been achieved had the Performance Ratio
been equal to the Performance Ratio Guarantee, given the actual Solar
Resource conditions during such Contract Year.

6.3 CAP ON LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

The aggregate Liquidated Damages payable by the Seller under Sections 6.1
and 6.2 in any Contract Year shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the
Seller's total tariff revenue for such Contract Year (the "LD Cap"). The
Parties agree and acknowledge that the Liquidated Damages amounts specified
herein represent a reasonable pre-estimate of the Buyer's likely loss and
are not penalties.

6.4 MEASUREMENT PERIOD

Performance shall be assessed annually, based on each Contract Year. The
Seller shall provide the Buyer with quarterly performance reports indicating
the Facility's Availability, Performance Ratio, energy output, and
comparison with expectations.

6.5 SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE

(a) Not less than sixty (60) days prior to the start of each Contract Year,
the Seller shall submit to the Buyer a proposed annual maintenance schedule
identifying all planned maintenance activities and their expected duration.

(b) The Buyer shall review the proposed schedule and may request reasonable
modifications to minimize the impact on energy delivery, and the Seller
shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate such requests.

(c) Scheduled Maintenance shall be planned during periods of lower solar
irradiance (where commercially feasible) and shall not exceed a cumulative
total of one hundred and seventy-five (175) hours per Contract Year without
the Buyer's prior written consent.

               ARTICLE 7: CURTAILMENT
================================================================================

7.1 TYPES OF CURTAILMENT

(a) Grid Curtailment: Any Curtailment directed by the Grid Operator or the
Buyer due to grid congestion, grid security concerns, system balancing
requirements, or Emergency conditions ("Grid Curtailment"). Grid Curtailment
shall be a Compensable Curtailment.

(b) Economic Curtailment: Any Curtailment directed by the Buyer for economic
reasons, including without limitation merit-order dispatch decisions or to
manage oversupply conditions ("Economic Curtailment"). Economic Curtailment
shall be a Compensable Curtailment.

(c) Force Majeure Curtailment: Any Curtailment resulting from a Force Majeure
Event ("Force Majeure Curtailment"). Force Majeure Curtailment shall not be
a Compensable Curtailment, and neither Party shall be liable for any loss
or damage arising therefrom, subject to Article 11.

7.2 DEEMED GENERATION CALCULATION

For purposes of calculating compensation for Compensable Curtailment, the
Deemed Generation during the curtailed period shall be calculated as follows:

Deemed Generation (MWh) = Facility Capacity (MW) x PR x GHI_actual /
GHI_STC x Hours of Curtailment x (1 - Degradation Factor)

Where:
- PR = the average Performance Ratio of the Facility during the most recent
  twelve (12) month period;
- GHI_actual = the actual Global Horizontal Irradiance measured at the
  Facility's weather stations during the curtailed period;
- GHI_STC = 1,000 W/m2 (Standard Test Conditions);
- Degradation Factor = the cumulative degradation factor applicable to the
  relevant Contract Year as specified in Schedule 1.

7.3 COMPENSATION FOR GRID CURTAILMENT

For Grid Curtailment, the Buyer shall pay the Seller an amount equal to
ninety percent (90%) of the product of the Deemed Generation and the
applicable Base Tariff for the relevant Contract Year.

7.4 COMPENSATION FOR ECONOMIC CURTAILMENT

For Economic Curtailment, the Buyer shall pay the Seller an amount equal to
one hundred percent (100%) of the product of the Deemed Generation and the
applicable Base Tariff for the relevant Contract Year.

7.5 CURTAILMENT LIMITS

(a) The aggregate Compensable Curtailment in any Contract Year shall not
exceed five hundred (500) hours. If the aggregate Compensable Curtailment
exceeds five hundred (500) hours, the Seller shall have the right to
terminate this Agreement upon one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice,
subject to payment of the Termination Payment by the Buyer.

(b) The Seller shall comply with all Dispatch Instructions issued by the
Grid Operator or the Buyer promptly and in accordance with the Grid Code,
regardless of whether such Dispatch Instructions constitute Compensable
Curtailment or Force Majeure Curtailment.

               ARTICLE 8: TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

8.1 GENERAL

The Facility shall be designed, engineered, constructed, and commissioned
in accordance with the Technical Specifications set forth in Schedule 1 and
all Applicable Laws, the Grid Code, and Good Utility Practice.

8.2 SOLAR MODULES

The Facility shall utilize monocrystalline bifacial solar photovoltaic
modules with the following minimum specifications:
(a) Module rated power: not less than 625 Wp per module;
(b) Module efficiency: not less than 21.5% under Standard Test Conditions;
(c) Bifaciality factor: not less than 70%;
(d) Temperature coefficient of power: not worse than -0.34%/degC;
(e) Module warranty: 12 years product warranty, 30 years linear power output
    warranty (minimum 87.4% at year 25);
(f) Certification: IEC 61215, IEC 61730, UL 61730.

8.3 INVERTERS

The Facility shall utilize string inverters with the following minimum
specifications:
(a) Rated AC output power: 3.2 MW per inverter;
(b) Maximum efficiency: not less than 99.0%;
(c) Euro-weighted efficiency: not less than 98.5%;
(d) Power factor control range: 0.9 leading to 0.9 lagging;
(e) Grid support functions: frequency response, voltage regulation, reactive
    power control, fault ride-through capability;
(f) Communication: MODBUS TCP/IP, IEC 61850 compatible.

8.4 TRACKING SYSTEM

The Facility shall utilize single-axis horizontal tracking systems with the
following minimum specifications:
(a) Tracking range: +/- 60 degrees from horizontal;
(b) Tracking algorithm: astronomical with backtracking and terrain-following;
(c) Wind stow position: 0 degrees (horizontal) at wind speeds exceeding
    22 m/s;
(d) Motor type: brushless DC or AC servo;
(e) Design wind speed: 150 km/h (3-second gust).

8.5 ELECTRICAL COLLECTION AND TRANSMISSION

(a) The Facility shall have a 33 kV medium-voltage collection system
connecting the inverter stations to the main step-up substation.
(b) The step-up substation shall include two (2) 33/132 kV power
transformers, each rated at not less than 140 MVA (ONAN/ONAF), with
appropriate switchgear, protection, and control systems.
(c) The 132 kV connection from the step-up substation to the Delivery Point
at the Al Dhafra 132 kV Substation shall be via underground cable or
overhead line as determined by the Grid Operator and approved by the Buyer.

          for harmonic distortion, voltage flicker, and other power quality

               ARTICLE 11: FORCE MAJEURE
================================================================================

11.1 DEFINITION

"Force Majeure" or "Force Majeure Event" means any event or circumstance, or
combination of events or circumstances, that: (a) is beyond the reasonable
control of the affected Party; (b) could not have been prevented or avoided
by the affected Party through the exercise of reasonable diligence and Good
Utility Practice; and (c) directly and materially prevents or delays the
affected Party from performing one or more of its material obligations under
this Agreement.

11.2 FORCE MAJEURE EVENTS

Without limiting the generality of Section 11.1, Force Majeure Events shall
include the following, subject to the conditions set forth in Section 11.1:

(a) Natural Force Majeure Events:
    (i) earthquake, tsunami, volcanic eruption, landslide, or subsidence;
    (ii) flood, storm, cyclone, hurricane, or typhoon of an intensity
         exceeding a 1-in-50-year recurrence interval;
    (iii) sandstorm or dust storm persisting for more than seventy-two (72)
          consecutive hours at the Site, as measured by the Facility's
          weather monitoring equipment;
    (iv) lightning strike causing direct physical damage to the Facility;
    (v) epidemic, pandemic, or plague declared by the World Health
         Organization or the UAE Ministry of Health;
    (vi) wildfire or brush fire threatening or damaging the Facility or
         restricting access thereto.

(b) Political Force Majeure Events:
    (i) war (declared or undeclared), invasion, armed conflict, blockade,
        embargo, or military action;
    (ii) revolution, insurrection, civil unrest, terrorism, or sabotage;
    (iii) act of any Governmental Authority, including nationalization,
          expropriation, confiscation, or sequestration of the Facility or
          any material assets of the Seller;
    (iv) imposition of economic sanctions or trade restrictions by the
         United Nations, the European Union, the United States, or the UAE;
    (v) nuclear, biological, or chemical contamination (unless caused by
        the affected Party).

(c) Grid Force Majeure Events:
    (i) failure or collapse of the electrical grid not caused by the Seller;
    (ii) disconnection of the Facility from the grid directed by the Grid
         Operator due to grid security concerns not attributable to the
         Seller.

11.3 EXCLUSIONS FROM FORCE MAJEURE

The following shall not constitute Force Majeure Events:
(a) any event or circumstance that could have been prevented or mitigated by
    the affected Party through the exercise of Good Utility Practice;
(b) normal weather conditions, including seasonal variations in Solar Resource;
(c) equipment failures or breakdowns attributable to defective design,
    manufacturing defects, or inadequate maintenance;
(d) economic hardship, including changes in market prices, currency
    fluctuations, loss of profitability, or inability to obtain financing;
(e) the failure of any subcontractor, supplier, or vendor of the affected
    Party to perform, unless such failure is itself caused by a Force
    Majeure Event;
(f) labor disputes, strikes, or lockouts limited to the Seller's workforce
    or the workforce of its contractors, unless such labor action is part of
    a general industry-wide or nationwide labor action.

11.4 FORCE MAJEURE NOTICE AND OBLIGATIONS

(a) The affected Party shall give written notice to the other Party within
five (5) Business Days of becoming aware of a Force Majeure Event, specifying
the nature, expected duration, and likely effect of the Force Majeure Event
on the affected Party's ability to perform its obligations.

(b) The affected Party shall use all reasonable efforts to mitigate the
effects of the Force Majeure Event and to resume performance of its
obligations as soon as reasonably practicable.

(c) The affected Party shall provide regular updates (not less than weekly)
to the other Party on the status of the Force Majeure Event and the
measures being taken to mitigate its effects.

11.5 CONSEQUENCES OF FORCE MAJEURE

(a) Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing any
of its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure or
delay is caused by a Force Majeure Event.

(b) If a Force Majeure Event prevents or delays the achievement of the
Commercial Operation Date, the Long-Stop Date shall be extended day-for-day
for the duration of the Force Majeure Event.

(c) If a Force Majeure Event continues for a period exceeding three hundred
and sixty-five (365) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this
Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice, subject to payment of the
Termination Payment as specified in Article 13.5.

               ARTICLE 13: TERMINATION
================================================================================

13.1 SELLER DEFAULTS

Each of the following shall constitute a "Seller Default":
(a) the Seller fails to achieve Commercial Operation by the Long-Stop Date
    (as may be extended);
(b) the Seller abandons the Facility for a period of sixty (60) consecutive
    days;
(c) the Seller fails to deliver Net Electrical Output to the Delivery Point
    for a continuous period of one hundred and eighty (180) days (other than
    due to Force Majeure, Compensable Curtailment, or approved Scheduled
    Maintenance);
(d) the Seller materially breaches any material provision of this Agreement
    and fails to cure such breach within the Cure Period;
(e) the Seller becomes insolvent, enters into bankruptcy, administration,
    or liquidation proceedings, or makes an assignment for the benefit of
    creditors;
(f) any representation or warranty made by the Seller in this Agreement
    proves to be materially false or misleading when made;
(g) the Seller fails to maintain the insurance required under Article 10
    and does not remedy such failure within thirty (30) days of notice.

13.2 BUYER DEFAULTS

Each of the following shall constitute a "Buyer Default":
(a) the Buyer fails to pay any undisputed amount due under this Agreement
    within sixty (60) days after the Payment Due Date;
(b) the Buyer materially breaches any material provision of this Agreement
    and fails to cure such breach within the Cure Period;
(c) the Buyer becomes insolvent, enters into bankruptcy, administration,
    or liquidation proceedings;
(d) the Buyer's electricity distribution license is revoked or terminated;
(e) any representation or warranty made by the Buyer proves to be materially
    false or misleading when made.

13.3 TERMINATION RIGHTS

(a) Upon the occurrence of a Seller Default, the Buyer may terminate this
Agreement by giving not less than sixty (60) days' written notice to the
Seller, provided that the Seller has been given the opportunity to cure such
default within the Cure Period specified in Section 13.4.

(b) Upon the occurrence of a Buyer Default, the Seller may terminate this
Agreement by giving not less than sixty (60) days' written notice to the
Buyer, provided that the Buyer has been given the opportunity to cure such
default within the Cure Period specified in Section 13.4.

13.4 CURE PERIOD

The Cure Period for any default (other than insolvency-related defaults,
for which no Cure Period applies) shall be sixty (60) days from the date
of receipt of written notice specifying the default, provided that if the
defaulting Party has commenced and is diligently pursuing a cure within
such sixty (60) day period, the Cure Period shall be extended to a maximum
of one hundred and twenty (120) days.

13.5 TERMINATION PAYMENTS

(a) Upon termination for Seller Default, the Seller shall pay to the Buyer
a Termination Payment equal to the aggregate of:
    (i) the cost to the Buyer of procuring replacement capacity and energy
        from alternative sources for the unexpired portion of the Term;
    (ii) any outstanding Liquidated Damages; and
    (iii) the Buyer's reasonable legal and administrative costs.

(b) Upon termination for Buyer Default, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller a
Termination Payment equal to the aggregate of:
    (i) the net present value (discounted at SOFR plus 1%) of the Seller's
        expected tariff revenue for the unexpired portion of the Term, less
        the Seller's avoided costs;
    (ii) the outstanding balance of the Seller's project financing; and
    (iii) the Seller's reasonable legal and administrative costs.

13.6 TRANSFER OF ASSETS

Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Buyer shall have the
option (but not the obligation) to acquire the Facility at a price equal to
the fair market value of the Facility as determined by an independent valuer
agreed upon by the Parties.

Substation Rating: 2 x 140 MVA, 33/132 kV
Transformer Type: ONAN/ONAF, copper wound, mineral oil filled
Cooling Type: Forced air cooling (ONAF mode)
On-Load Tap Changer: +/- 10% in 17 steps
132 kV Switchgear: GIS (Gas Insulated Switchgear), SF6
33 kV Switchgear: Metal-clad, vacuum circuit breakers
Protection System: Distance protection, differential protection,
    overcurrent protection, earth fault protection, busbar protection
SCADA System: Fully integrated, IEC 61850 compliant
Reactive Power Compensation: Static VAR Compensator (SVC), +/- 50 MVAR

Year 1: 0.00% (no degradation during first year)
Year 2: 2.00% (initial light-induced degradation)
Year 3 through Year 25: 0.45% per year (linear annual degradation)

    2.00% + (23 x 0.45%) = 2.00% + 10.35% = 12.35%
    Guaranteed minimum power output at Year 25: 87.65% of nameplate

- Pyranometer (Class A, ISO 9060): measuring GHI
- Pyrheliometer with solar tracker: measuring DNI
- Pyranometer (tilted): measuring plane-of-array irradiance
- Ambient temperature sensor (ventilated)
- Module back-surface temperature sensor
- Wind speed and direction sensor (anemometer and wind vane)
- Barometric pressure sensor
- Relative humidity sensor
- Rain gauge
- Soiling sensor (DustIQ or equivalent)

Data logging interval: 1 minute
Data transmission: Real-time via fiber optic to central SCADA
