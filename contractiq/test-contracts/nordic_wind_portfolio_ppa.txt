RECITALS

WHEREAS the Seller is developing and will own and operate a portfolio of three
(3) onshore wind farms located across Sweden and Finland with a combined
nameplate capacity of 420 MW (the "Facilities"), consisting of:

     (a)  Lapland Wind Farm (180 MW), located in Jokkmokk Municipality,
          Norrbotten County, Sweden ("Facility A");
     (b)  Vasterbotten Wind Farm (140 MW), located in Skelleftea Municipality,
          Vasterbotten County, Sweden ("Facility B"); and
     (c)  Ostrobothnia Wind Farm (100 MW), located in Vaasa sub-region,
          Ostrobothnia, Finland ("Facility C");

WHEREAS the Buyer desires to procure renewable electricity on a virtual
(financial settlement) basis to match its Nordic data center electricity
consumption with renewable generation, supporting the Buyer's corporate
sustainability commitments and RE100 membership obligations;

WHEREAS the Parties wish to enter into this Power Purchase Agreement (the
"Agreement") setting out the terms under which the Seller shall sell and the
Buyer shall purchase the Contracted Output and associated Environmental
Attributes from the Facilities;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein
contained, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 4: OVERVIEW AND STRUCTURE
================================================================================

4.1  RULE OV-1 (Agreement Structure): This Agreement is a virtual (financial)
     power purchase agreement under which no physical delivery of electricity
     occurs between the Parties. The Seller shall sell the Contracted Output
     into the Nord Pool day-ahead market (Elspot) and, where applicable, the
     intraday market (Elbas). Financial settlement between the Parties shall
     be calculated as the difference between the Contract Price determined
     under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1) and the Reference Price determined under
     Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2), applied to the Contracted Output measured under
     Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1), and settled in accordance with Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: PM-1, PM-2, VT-1, PY-1.

4.2  RULE OV-2 (Portfolio Aggregation): The Contracted Output from all three
     Facilities shall be aggregated for the purposes of financial settlement
     under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) and the Annual Delivery Guarantee under
     Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3). However, GoO delivery under Rule CD-1
     (Article 6.1) and balancing responsibility under Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4)
     shall be tracked on a per-Facility basis. In the event of partial
     portfolio operation (e.g., pre-COD for one Facility), settlement under
     Rule PY-1 shall apply only to operating Facilities, and the Minimum
     Annual Delivery under Rule VT-2 shall be pro-rated accordingly.

     Linked Rules: PY-1, VT-2, CD-1, ED-3, EV-1.

4.3  RULE OV-3 (Regulatory Framework): This Agreement is governed by and
     shall be interpreted in accordance with the electricity market regulations
     of the Kingdom of Sweden and the Republic of Finland, the EU Renewable
     Energy Directive (2018/2001), and Nord Pool's applicable trading rules.
     Regulatory changes affecting the pricing mechanism under Rule PM-1
     (Article 8.1) or the GoO regime under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1) shall be
     addressed through the regulatory change provisions of Rule EV-6
     (Article 10.7).

     Linked Rules: PM-1, CD-1, EV-6.


================================================================================
ARTICLE 5: ELECTRICITY DELIVERY
================================================================================

5.1  RULE ED-1 (Delivery Obligation): The Seller shall cause each Facility to
     generate and deliver electricity to the relevant Grid Delivery Point on a
     continuous basis, subject only to (a) scheduled maintenance notified under
     Rule ED-4 (Article 5.5), (b) Force Majeure Events under Rule EV-4
     (Article 10.5), and (c) curtailment events under Rule ED-2
     (Article 5.3). The Seller shall operate each Facility in accordance with
     Good Utility Practice and shall not voluntarily curtail production except
     as permitted under Rule ED-2 (Article 5.3).

     Linked Rules: ED-2, ED-4, EV-4, VT-1.

5.2  RULE ED-2 (Grid Curtailment and Dispatch): Where a transmission system
     operator (Svenska Kraftnat or Fingrid) issues a curtailment instruction,
     the Seller shall comply with such instruction and the curtailed volumes
     shall be excluded from the Contracted Output for purposes of settlement
     under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1). Curtailed volumes shall, however, count
     toward the Minimum Annual Delivery calculation under Rule VT-2
     (Article 9.3) at the deemed production rate determined by the average
     output of the affected Facility during the four (4) hours immediately
     preceding the curtailment event, provided that the Seller can demonstrate
     the curtailment was not caused by the Seller's own acts or omissions.

     Linked Rules: PY-1, VT-2, ED-1.

5.3  RULE ED-3 (Balancing Responsibility): The Seller shall act as or procure
     a Balancing Responsible Party for each Facility and shall bear all costs
     associated with imbalance settlement in the relevant bidding zone (SE1,
     SE2, SE3, or FI). The Seller shall submit production forecasts to the
     relevant BRP no later than 12:00 CET D-1 for day-ahead nomination and
     shall use commercially reasonable efforts to update intraday positions via
     Elbas. All balancing costs and revenues shall be for the Seller's sole
     account and shall not affect the Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1) or the Contract Price under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, PM-1, ED-1, VT-3.

5.4  RULE ED-4 (Scheduled Maintenance): The Seller shall provide the Buyer
     with a rolling twelve (12) month maintenance schedule, updated quarterly,
     and shall provide no less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice of
     any scheduled maintenance expected to reduce output by more than 20% of
     nameplate capacity for any individual Facility. Scheduled maintenance
     shall be planned outside the peak wind season (October through March) to
     the extent commercially practicable. During scheduled maintenance, the
     Seller's delivery obligation under Rule ED-1 (Article 5.1) shall be
     suspended for the affected Facility, and maintenance hours shall be
     excluded from the capacity factor calculation under Rule VT-3
     (Article 9.4).

     Linked Rules: ED-1, VT-3, VT-2.

5.5  RULE ED-5 (Metering and Data): Net generation at each Grid Delivery Point
     shall be measured by revenue-grade metering equipment compliant with IEC
     62053-22 Class 0.5S accuracy standards. Hourly meter data shall be
     transmitted electronically to both Parties and the settlement agent no
     later than 5 business days after the end of each Settlement Period.
     Metered data constitutes the basis for the Contracted Output under
     Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1) and the hourly settlement calculation under
     Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1). Any metering discrepancy exceeding 0.5% shall
     be resolved pursuant to the dispute resolution procedures in Article 12.

     Linked Rules: VT-1, PY-1, VT-3.


================================================================================
ARTICLE 6: CERTIFICATE DELIVERY
================================================================================

6.1  RULE CD-1 (GoO Delivery Obligation): The Seller shall transfer to the
     Buyer Guarantees of Origin corresponding to 100% of the Contracted Output
     for each Settlement Period, via the AIB registry system (specifically, the
     Swedish Cesar registry for Facilities A and B and the Finnish Grexel
     registry for Facility C). GoOs shall identify the technology (onshore
     wind), the specific Facility, and the production period in accordance with
     the European Energy Certificate System (EECS) Rules. The Seller warrants
     that all GoOs are genuine, first-transfer, and have not been used for
     any other disclosure or compliance purpose, as represented in Article 3.2.

     Linked Rules: VT-1, OV-2, CD-2, CD-3.

6.2  RULE CD-2 (Delivery Schedule): GoOs shall be delivered quarterly, no
     later than forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter
     (each such deadline, a "Certificate Delivery Date"). Delivery shall be
     deemed complete upon the GoOs being credited to the Buyer's designated
     account in the relevant AIB registry. Quarterly bundling means that GoOs
     for January-March production are delivered by 15 May, April-June by
     15 August, July-September by 15 November, and October-December by
     15 February of the following year. Late delivery shall incur the penalty
     specified in Rule PY-4 (Article 7.5).

     Linked Rules: CD-1, PY-4, VT-1.

6.3  RULE CD-3 (Certificate Shortfall): If the Seller is unable to deliver
     GoOs corresponding to the full Contracted Output for any quarter, the
     Seller shall, at its own cost, procure replacement GoOs of equivalent
     technology (onshore wind) and vintage (same calendar year) from Nordic
     bidding zones (NO, SE, FI, DK) within thirty (30) days of the applicable
     Certificate Delivery Date. If replacement GoOs cannot be sourced, the
     Seller shall pay the Buyer a certificate shortfall payment equal to the
     volume of undelivered GoOs multiplied by the average GoO market price
     published by ECOHZ or Commerg for the relevant vintage and technology,
     plus a 15% premium. This shortfall payment is in addition to, and not in
     lieu of, any delivery shortfall payments under Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4).

     Linked Rules: CD-1, CD-2, PY-3, PY-4, VT-2.


================================================================================
ARTICLE 7: PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT
================================================================================

7.1  RULE PY-1 (Settlement Calculation): For each Measurement Period (h) during
     a Settlement Period, the Settlement Amount shall be calculated as follows:

          Settlement Amount(h) = Contracted Output(h) x (Contract Price(h)
                                 - Reference Price(h))

     where:
     - Contracted Output(h) is the metered net generation in MWh for hour (h)
       as determined under Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1) and verified under
       Rule ED-5 (Article 5.6);
     - Contract Price(h) is the price applicable for hour (h) as determined
       under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1) and Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3);
     - Reference Price(h) is the Nord Pool System Price for the relevant
       bidding zone and hour (h) as determined under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2).

     If the Settlement Amount is positive, the Buyer shall pay the Seller. If
     negative, the Seller shall pay the Buyer. The aggregate monthly
     Settlement Amount is the sum of all hourly Settlement Amounts for the
     Settlement Period.

     Linked Rules: VT-1, ED-5, PM-1, PM-2, PM-3.

7.2  RULE PY-2 (Invoicing and Payment Terms): The Seller shall issue an
     invoice to the Buyer within ten (10) business days after receipt of final
     metered data for each Settlement Period. Each invoice shall contain a
     detailed hourly breakdown of Contracted Output, Contract Price, Reference
     Price, and Settlement Amount per Measurement Period. Payment shall be due
     within twenty (20) business days of invoice date, by wire transfer to the
     account specified in Schedule C. Late payment shall bear interest at
     EURIBOR 3-month plus 2.00% per annum, calculated on a daily basis.
     Invoicing is dependent on the metering data provision under Rule ED-5
     (Article 5.6) and the price publication under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, ED-5, PM-2.

7.3  RULE PY-3 (Delivery Shortfall Payment): If the aggregate Contracted
     Output for any Contract Year falls below the Minimum Annual Delivery
     specified in Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3), the Seller shall pay to the Buyer a
     Delivery Shortfall Payment calculated as:

          Shortfall Payment = (Minimum Annual Delivery - Actual Annual Output)
                              x Base Price x 0.50

     where Base Price is as defined in Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1). The Delivery
     Shortfall Payment shall be invoiced by the Buyer as part of the Annual
     True-Up under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.3). Volumes excluded due to Force
     Majeure under Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5) and grid curtailment deemed under
     Rule ED-2 (Article 5.3) shall be added back to Actual Annual Output for
     purposes of this calculation.

     Linked Rules: VT-2, PM-1, EV-2, EV-4, ED-2.

7.4  RULE PY-4 (Certificate Delivery Penalty): If the Seller fails to deliver
     GoOs by the Certificate Delivery Date specified in Rule CD-2
     (Article 6.3), the Seller shall pay a late delivery penalty of
     EUR 0.50 per MWh per week of delay (or part thereof), applied to the
     volume of GoOs not yet delivered. This penalty shall accrue from the
     Certificate Delivery Date until the earlier of (a) actual delivery or
     (b) the date on which the certificate shortfall payment under Rule CD-3
     (Article 6.4) becomes payable. The penalty under this Rule PY-4 is
     separate from and in addition to the shortfall payment under Rule CD-3.

     Linked Rules: CD-2, CD-3, CD-1, PY-2.

7.5  RULE PY-5 (Security and Credit Support): The Seller shall maintain, for
     the duration of the Term, a performance bond or letter of credit in the
     amount of EUR 5,000,000, issued by a financial institution with a minimum
     credit rating of A- (S&P) or equivalent. The Buyer may draw upon such
     security in the event of (a) non-payment of any Settlement Amount due
     under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) exceeding thirty (30) days past due,
     (b) non-delivery of GoOs exceeding ninety (90) days past the Certificate
     Delivery Date under Rule CD-2 (Article 6.3), or (c) Seller Event of
     Default under Rule EV-5 (Article 10.6).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, CD-2, EV-5.


================================================================================
ARTICLE 8: PRICE INDEXATION AND MARKET DATA
================================================================================

8.1  RULE PM-1 (Contract Price - Hybrid Floor): The Contract Price for each
     Measurement Period shall be no less than the Base Price of EUR 32.00 per
     MWh (the "Floor Price"). The Floor Price shall be adjusted annually on
     each anniversary of the first COD by the Harmonized Index of Consumer
     Prices (HICP) for the Euro Area as published by Eurostat, applied on a
     cumulative compounding basis from the Effective Date. The Floor Price
     constitutes the minimum payment obligation and interacts with the upside
     sharing mechanism defined in Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3) and the settlement
     calculation in Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: PM-3, PY-1, PY-3, EV-3.

8.2  RULE PM-2 (Reference Price): The Reference Price for each Measurement
     Period shall be the Nord Pool day-ahead system price for the bidding zone
     in which the relevant Facility is located (SE1 for Facility A, SE2 for
     Facility B, FI for Facility C), as published by Nord Pool AS on its
     official platform (www.nordpoolgroup.com) by 14:00 CET D+1. Where
     Facilities span multiple bidding zones, the Reference Price for portfolio
     settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) shall be the production-weighted
     average of the applicable zone prices, weighted by metered output per
     Facility per Measurement Period as reported under Rule VT-1
     (Article 9.1).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, VT-1, PM-1, ED-3.

8.3  RULE PM-3 (Upside Sharing Mechanism): When the Reference Price for any
     Measurement Period exceeds the Cap Price of EUR 55.00 per MWh, the
     Contract Price for that hour shall be calculated as:

          Contract Price(h) = Floor Price + 0.40 x (Reference Price(h)
                              - Cap Price)

     The Buyer's Upside Share is forty percent (40%) of the margin above the
     Cap Price. When the Reference Price is between the Floor Price and the
     Cap Price, the Contract Price equals the Floor Price (i.e., the Buyer
     benefits from the entire spread). When the Reference Price is below the
     Floor Price, the Contract Price remains at the Floor Price (i.e., the
     Seller receives a top-up payment from the Buyer equal to the difference,
     as calculated under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1)). This tiered structure
     ensures the Seller always receives at least the Floor Price while sharing
     supernormal returns with the Buyer, and is subject to reset under
     Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4).

     Linked Rules: PM-1, PM-2, PY-1, EV-3.

8.4  RULE PM-4 (Market Data Fallback): In the event that Nord Pool fails to
     publish the day-ahead system price for any Measurement Period, the
     Reference Price shall be determined as follows in order of priority:
     (a) the EPEX SPOT Nordic day-ahead price for the equivalent zone and
     hour; (b) the average of the day-ahead prices published by the two
     nearest bidding zones for the same hour; (c) the average Reference Price
     for the same hour across the preceding seven (7) calendar days. If none
     of the foregoing are available, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith
     a substitute reference price. Any fallback determination affects the
     Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) and shall be documented
     in the monthly settlement report under Rule PY-2 (Article 7.2).

     Linked Rules: PM-2, PY-1, PY-2, VT-1.


================================================================================
ARTICLE 9: VOLUME AND TIMESERIES
================================================================================

9.1  RULE VT-1 (Hourly Production Measurement): The Contracted Output for each
     Measurement Period shall be the net electrical energy (in MWh, to three
     decimal places) injected at the Grid Delivery Point of each Facility, as
     recorded by the revenue meters specified in Rule ED-5 (Article 5.6),
     adjusted for (a) transformer losses between generator terminals and the
     Grid Delivery Point (estimated at 1.5%), and (b) any parasitic
     consumption by the Facility's auxiliary systems. Hourly data shall form
     the basis for settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1), the Reference
     Price weighting under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2), and the GoO volume
     calculation under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1).

     Linked Rules: ED-5, PY-1, PM-2, CD-1.

9.2  RULE VT-2 (Annual Delivery Guarantee): The Seller guarantees a Minimum
     Annual Delivery equal to eighty-five percent (85%) of the aggregate P50
     Estimate for all operating Facilities (the "Minimum Annual Delivery").
     Based on the P50 Estimates in Schedule B, the initial Minimum Annual
     Delivery is:

          Facility A (180 MW):  P50 = 540 GWh/yr  ->  Minimum = 459 GWh/yr
          Facility B (140 MW):  P50 = 380 GWh/yr  ->  Minimum = 323 GWh/yr
          Facility C (100 MW):  P50 = 260 GWh/yr  ->  Minimum = 221 GWh/yr
          Portfolio Total:      P50 = 1,180 GWh/yr -> Minimum = 1,003 GWh/yr

     Failure to meet the Minimum Annual Delivery triggers the Delivery
     Shortfall Payment under Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4), subject to the
     exclusions for Force Majeure under Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5) and grid
     curtailment under Rule ED-2 (Article 5.3). The Annual Delivery Guarantee
     shall be reconciled as part of the Annual True-Up under Rule EV-2
     (Article 10.3).

     Linked Rules: PY-3, EV-4, ED-2, EV-2, VT-1, ED-4.

9.3  RULE VT-3 (Capacity Factor Tracking): The Seller shall report monthly
     the net capacity factor for each Facility, calculated as:

          NCF = Actual Output / (Nameplate Capacity x Hours in Period
                - Maintenance Hours per Rule ED-4 (Article 5.5))

     If the trailing twelve-month NCF for any Facility falls below 22%, the
     Buyer may request a Technical Review pursuant to Article 11. The capacity
     factor is used to assess performance relative to the P50 Estimates
     underpinning the Annual Delivery Guarantee in Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3)
     and affects the metering reconciliation under Rule ED-5 (Article 5.6).

     Linked Rules: ED-4, VT-2, ED-5, VT-1.

9.4  RULE VT-4 (Production Forecast Provision): The Seller shall provide the
     Buyer with (a) a rolling 48-hour production forecast updated every 6
     hours, (b) a monthly production forecast issued by the 25th of the
     preceding month, and (c) an annual production forecast issued by
     1 December of the preceding year. Forecasts shall be provided at the
     Facility level and aggregated portfolio level. While forecasts are
     provided for information purposes only and do not modify the Seller's
     delivery obligations under Rule ED-1 (Article 5.1), persistent deviation
     of actuals from forecasts exceeding 30% on a quarterly basis shall
     trigger a good-faith consultation between the Parties. Forecast data
     feeds into the balancing nominations under Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4).

     Linked Rules: ED-1, ED-3, VT-1, VT-2.

ARTICLE 10: EVENTS
================================================================================

10.1 RULE EV-1 (Commercial Operation Date Milestones): Each Facility shall
     achieve COD upon satisfaction of all of the following conditions:
     (a) completion of grid connection and energization tests; (b) issuance of
     a Provisional Acceptance Certificate by the EPC contractor; (c) successful
     completion of a 240-hour reliability test at no less than 95% availability;
     and (d) receipt of all necessary operating permits. The expected COD dates
     are: Facility A - Q2 2026, Facility B - Q3 2026, Facility C - Q4 2026.
     Upon COD, delivery obligations under Rule ED-1 (Article 5.1) commence and
     the Contracted Output begins to be measured under Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1)
     for settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: ED-1, VT-1, PY-1, OV-2, VT-2.

10.2 RULE EV-2 (Annual True-Up): Within ninety (90) days after the end of each
     Contract Year, the Parties shall conduct an Annual True-Up reconciliation,
     which shall include: (a) verification of aggregate Contracted Output
     against the Minimum Annual Delivery under Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3);
     (b) calculation and settlement of any Delivery Shortfall Payment under
     Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4); (c) reconciliation of GoO deliveries against
     metered output under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1); (d) recalculation of the
     HICP-adjusted Floor Price under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1) for the
     forthcoming Contract Year; and (e) review of capacity factor performance
     under Rule VT-3 (Article 9.4). The True-Up statement shall be binding
     absent manifest error.

     Linked Rules: VT-2, PY-3, CD-1, PM-1, VT-3.

10.3 RULE EV-3 (Five-Year Price Reset): On the fifth (5th) and tenth (10th)
     anniversaries of the first COD (each a "Reset Date"), either Party may
     request a review of the Base Price and Cap Price. The reset shall be
     determined by an independent market consultant appointed by mutual
     agreement, taking into account: (a) the average Nord Pool system price
     over the preceding 36 months as published under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2);
     (b) changes in the levelized cost of onshore wind energy; (c) prevailing
     corporate PPA market pricing in the Nordics. The adjusted Base Price shall
     not deviate by more than +/-15% from the then-current Base Price, and the
     Cap Price shall be adjusted proportionally. If the Parties cannot agree
     on the reset within 120 days of the Reset Date, the existing prices under
     Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1) and Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3) shall continue to
     apply unchanged.

     Linked Rules: PM-1, PM-2, PM-3, PY-1.

10.4 RULE EV-4 (Force Majeure): A "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond
     the reasonable control of the affected Party, including but not limited to:
     (a) grid curtailment ordered by Svenska Kraftnat or Fingrid for system
     security reasons (distinct from economic curtailment under Rule ED-2
     (Article 5.3)); (b) extreme weather conditions where ambient temperature
     falls below -35 degrees Celsius for more than 48 consecutive hours,
     causing turbine cold-weather shutdown; (c) natural disasters, war,
     terrorism, or pandemic; (d) changes in law that make performance illegal.
     During a Force Majeure Event, the affected Party's obligations under
     Rule ED-1 (Article 5.1) and Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3) shall be suspended
     to the extent affected. Force Majeure volumes are excluded from the
     Delivery Shortfall calculation under Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4) and shall
     not count against the Minimum Annual Delivery under Rule VT-2.

     Linked Rules: ED-1, ED-2, VT-2, PY-3.

10.5 RULE EV-5 (Termination Triggers): Either Party may terminate this
     Agreement by written notice upon the occurrence of any of the following:
     (a) a material breach by the other Party that remains uncured for sixty
     (60) days after written notice; (b) insolvency, bankruptcy, or
     administration of the other Party; (c) failure of any Facility to achieve
     COD by the Longstop Date (as may be extended under Article 2.3);
     (d) aggregate Contracted Output falling below seventy percent (70%) of
     the Minimum Annual Delivery under Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3) for two (2)
     consecutive Contract Years, after adjusting for Force Majeure under
     Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5); (e) non-payment of Settlement Amounts under
     Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) exceeding EUR 2,000,000 in aggregate and
     remaining unpaid for sixty (60) days. Upon termination, the termination
     payment shall be calculated as the present value of the remaining expected
     Settlement Amounts for the unexpired Term, discounted at the EUR mid-swap
     rate plus 1.00%.

     Linked Rules: VT-2, EV-4, PY-1, EV-1, PY-5.

10.6 RULE EV-6 (Regulatory Change): If a change in applicable law, regulation,
     or government policy materially affects the economic balance of this
     Agreement (including but not limited to changes to the GoO regime, Nordic
     market design, or the introduction of capacity mechanisms), either Party
     may request renegotiation of affected terms within sixty (60) days of the
     change taking effect. Affected terms include the pricing mechanism under
     Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1), the certificate delivery framework under
     Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1), and the balancing responsibilities under
     Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4). If the Parties cannot reach agreement within
     one hundred and twenty (120) days, either Party may invoke the dispute
     resolution mechanism in Article 12 or, if the change renders the Agreement
     fundamentally uneconomic, terminate under Rule EV-5 (Article 10.6)
     clause (d).

     Linked Rules: PM-1, CD-1, ED-3, EV-5, OV-3.
