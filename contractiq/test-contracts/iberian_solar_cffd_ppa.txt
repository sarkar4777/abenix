Strike Price and the variable market reference price as published by the
Iberian electricity market operator (OMIE);

ARTICLE 4: OVERVIEW AND STRUCTURE
================================================================================

4.1  RULE OV-1 (CfD Mechanism): This Agreement is a financial Contract for
     Difference under which no physical delivery of electricity occurs between
     the Parties. The Seller sells the Facilities' output into the OMIE
     day-ahead market independently. The Parties settle financially based on
     the difference between the Strike Price defined in Rule PM-1
     (Article 8.1) and the Reference Price defined in Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2),
     applied to the Contracted Volume determined under Rule VT-1
     (Article 9.1). If the Reference Price exceeds the Strike Price, the
     Seller pays the Buyer the difference. If the Strike Price exceeds the
     Reference Price, the Buyer pays the Seller the difference. Settlement
     mechanics are specified in Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: PM-1, PM-2, VT-1, PY-1.

4.2  RULE OV-2 (Shape Risk Allocation): The Buyer bears the capture rate risk
     inherent in solar generation. Settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1)
     is based on the Plant-Weighted Average Price calculated under Rule PM-3
     (Article 8.3), not the baseload average OMIE price. This means the
     effective Reference Price reflects the actual hourly price profile during
     which the Facilities generate, which for solar assets is typically lower
     than the flat baseload average due to the solar merit-order effect. The
     Buyer acknowledges this risk and has reflected it in the Strike Price
     agreed under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1). The Capture Rate is tracked and
     reported under Rule VT-3 (Article 9.4).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, PM-3, PM-1, VT-3.

4.3  RULE OV-3 (Regulatory Framework): This Agreement is subject to Spanish
     electricity market regulations, including but not limited to: (a) Ley
     24/2013 del Sector Electrico; (b) Real Decreto 413/2014 on renewable
     energy remuneration; (c) OMIE market rules; and (d) EU Directive
     2018/2001 on renewable energy. Regulatory changes are addressed under
     Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4). The Parties acknowledge that the Spanish
     energy regulatory landscape is subject to ongoing reform and have
     incorporated specific protections in Rule EV-3 to address potential
     impacts on the pricing under Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1), the certificate
     regime under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1), and the volume measurement
     framework under Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1).

     Linked Rules: EV-3, PM-1, CD-1, VT-1.

ARTICLE 5: ELECTRICITY DELIVERY
================================================================================

5.1  RULE ED-1 (Generation and Market Sale): The Seller shall operate the
     Facilities in accordance with Good Industry Practice and shall sell all
     generated electricity into the OMIE day-ahead market. The Seller shall
     use commercially reasonable efforts to maximize generation subject to
     (a) equipment limitations, (b) grid constraints, (c) scheduled
     maintenance under Rule ED-4 (Article 5.5), (d) Force Majeure under
     Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5), and (e) the negative pricing curtailment
     provisions of Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4). As this is a financial CfD, the
     Seller retains full operational control over dispatch decisions, and the
     Buyer has no right to direct or constrain generation except as expressly
     provided in Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4).

     Linked Rules: ED-3, ED-4, EV-4, VT-1.

5.2  RULE ED-2 (Grid Connection and Curtailment): Each Facility is connected
     to the REE transmission network at the Grid Connection Points specified
     in Schedule A. In the event of grid curtailment ordered by REE (the
     Spanish TSO), curtailed volumes shall be excluded from the Contracted
     Volume for CfD settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1). However,
     curtailed volumes shall be included in the annual production assessment
     under Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3) at the deemed generation rate, calculated
     as the solar irradiance-based expected production for the curtailed
     hours using data from the Facility's pyranometer array, provided that
     the curtailment was not caused by the Seller's breach of grid code
     obligations. Grid curtailment data shall be reported to the Buyer
     monthly as part of the production report under Rule VT-4 (Article 9.5).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, VT-2, VT-4, ED-1.

5.3  RULE ED-3 (Negative Pricing Curtailment): When the OMIE day-ahead price
     as determined under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2) falls below negative twenty
     euros per megawatt-hour (-EUR 20.00/MWh) (the "Curtailment Threshold")
     for six (6) or more consecutive hours (a "Negative Price Period"), the
     Seller may, at its sole discretion, curtail generation during the
     Negative Price Period. Any volumes curtailed during a Negative Price
     Period shall be excluded from the Contracted Volume under Rule VT-1
     (Article 9.1) and shall not be subject to CfD settlement under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1). The Seller shall notify the Buyer within 24 hours of any
     curtailment decision under this Rule ED-3, specifying the hours affected
     and the estimated curtailed volume. For the avoidance of doubt, the
     Seller is not obligated to curtail during a Negative Price Period and may
     choose to continue generating, in which case normal settlement under
     Rule PY-1 shall apply to those hours. The six-hour consecutive threshold
     is calculated based on the final OMIE day-ahead prices published for the
     delivery day under Rule PM-2.

     Linked Rules: PM-2, VT-1, PY-1, ED-1, PM-4.

5.4  RULE ED-4 (Scheduled Maintenance): The Seller shall conduct scheduled
     maintenance in accordance with manufacturer recommendations and Good
     Industry Practice. The Seller shall provide the Buyer with: (a) an annual
     maintenance schedule by 1 December of each year for the following calendar
     year; (b) written notice at least twenty-one (21) days prior to any
     scheduled maintenance expected to reduce generation by more than 15% of
     peak capacity for any Facility. Scheduled maintenance shall be planned
     during months of lower irradiance (November through February) to the
     extent commercially practicable. Maintenance outage hours shall be
     excluded from the capacity factor and availability calculations under
     Rule VT-3 (Article 9.4) and the annual production assessment under
     Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3).

     Linked Rules: ED-1, VT-3, VT-2.

5.5  RULE ED-5 (Metering and Data Provision): Net generation at each Grid
     Connection Point shall be measured by fiscal metering equipment installed
     and maintained in accordance with Spanish metering regulations (ITC-BT-04
     and Real Decreto 1110/2007). Hourly production data shall be sourced from
     REE's ESIOS platform (Sistema de Informacion del Operador del Sistema)
     and cross-referenced with the Seller's SCADA system data. Monthly metering
     reports shall be delivered to the Buyer within seven (7) business days of
     the end of each Settlement Period. Metered data forms the basis for the
     Contracted Volume under Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1), the Plant-Weighted
     Average Price calculation under Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3), and the GoO
     volume reconciliation under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1). Metering disputes
     exceeding a threshold of 1.0% of monthly output shall be resolved under
     Article 12.

     Linked Rules: VT-1, PM-3, CD-1, PY-1.

ARTICLE 6: CERTIFICATE DELIVERY
================================================================================

6.1  RULE CD-1 (GoO Delivery Obligation): The Seller shall transfer to the
     Buyer Guarantees of Origin corresponding to 100% of the Contracted Volume
     for each Settlement Period, via the CNMC-administered GoO registry
     (Sistema de Garantias de Origen). GoOs shall identify the technology
     (solar photovoltaic), the specific Facility (identified by CIL code),
     and the production period. The Seller warrants exclusive transfer rights
     and confirms that the Facilities are not enrolled in any national support
     scheme that would preclude GoO issuance. The GoO delivery obligation is
     linked to the Contracted Volume determination under Rule VT-1
     (Article 9.1) and forms a core component of the Annual Reconciliation
     under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.2).

     Linked Rules: VT-1, EV-2, CD-2, CD-3, ED-5.

6.2  RULE CD-2 (Delivery Schedule and Vintage Bundling): GoOs shall be
     delivered annually, bundled by calendar year vintage. The Seller shall
     transfer all GoOs for a given calendar year to the Buyer's designated
     CNMC registry account no later than 31 March of the following calendar
     year (the "Certificate Delivery Deadline"). This annual delivery schedule
     reflects the CNMC's GoO issuance cycle, whereby GoOs are typically issued
     on a quarterly basis but may be subject to verification delays. The Seller
     shall use best efforts to deliver GoOs on a quarterly interim basis where
     the CNMC registry permits, but the binding delivery obligation is the
     annual Certificate Delivery Deadline. Late delivery penalties are
     specified in Rule PY-4 (Article 7.5).

     Linked Rules: CD-1, PY-4, VT-1, EV-2.

6.3  RULE CD-3 (Certificate Shortfall and Replacement): If the Seller is
     unable to deliver GoOs corresponding to the full Contracted Volume for
     any calendar year vintage by the Certificate Delivery Deadline under
     Rule CD-2 (Article 6.2), the Seller shall, within sixty (60) days: (a)
     procure replacement GoOs of equivalent technology (solar photovoltaic)
     and vintage (same calendar year) from Spanish or Portuguese generating
     facilities registered with CNMC or ERSE (the Portuguese regulator),
     respectively; or (b) if replacement GoOs are unavailable, pay the Buyer a
     certificate shortfall payment equal to the undelivered GoO volume
     multiplied by the average GoO auction price published by OMIP (Operador
     do Mercado Iberico de Energia - Polo Portugues) for Spanish solar GoOs
     of the relevant vintage, plus a twenty percent (20%) premium. The
     shortfall payment under this Rule CD-3 is additional to and independent
     from the CfD Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) and the
     late delivery penalty under Rule PY-4 (Article 7.5).

     Linked Rules: CD-2, CD-1, PY-1, PY-4, VT-2.

ARTICLE 7: PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT
================================================================================

7.1  RULE PY-1 (CfD Settlement Calculation): For each Settlement Period, the
     CfD Settlement Amount shall be calculated as follows:

          CfD Settlement Amount = Contracted Volume x (Strike Price
                                  - Plant-Weighted Average Price)

     where:
     - Contracted Volume is the total metered net generation (in MWh) across
       both Facilities for the Settlement Period, as determined under Rule VT-1
       (Article 9.1), excluding volumes curtailed under Rule ED-3
       (Article 5.4) and grid curtailment under Rule ED-2 (Article 5.3);
     - Strike Price is EUR 42.50/MWh as defined in Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1),
       subject to any adjustment under Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4);
     - Plant-Weighted Average Price is the production-weighted average OMIE
       day-ahead price, calculated under Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3).

     If the CfD Settlement Amount is positive, the Buyer shall pay the Seller
     (i.e., the market price was below the Strike Price). If negative, the
     Seller shall pay the Buyer (i.e., the market price exceeded the Strike
     Price). Settlement is on a monthly financial basis with no physical
     delivery component.

     Linked Rules: VT-1, ED-3, ED-2, PM-1, PM-3, EV-3.

7.2  RULE PY-2 (Invoicing and Payment Terms): The paying Party (as determined
     by the sign of the CfD Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1) shall issue a
     settlement statement to the other Party within fifteen (15) business days
     after receipt of final metered data for each Settlement Period. The
     settlement statement shall include: (a) hourly Contracted Volume by
     Facility; (b) hourly OMIE day-ahead prices; (c) Plant-Weighted Average
     Price calculation; (d) CfD Settlement Amount. Payment shall be due within
     twenty-five (25) business days of the settlement statement date, by SEPA
     credit transfer to the account specified in Schedule C. Late payments
     shall accrue interest at EURIBOR 6-month plus 2.50% per annum, calculated
     on an ACT/360 day-count basis. The invoicing process depends on timely
     metering data delivery under Rule ED-5 (Article 5.5) and price
     publication under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, ED-5, PM-2, PM-3.

7.3  RULE PY-3 (Annual Volume True-Up Payment): If the aggregate Contracted
     Volume for any calendar year falls below the Minimum Annual Production
     threshold specified in Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3), the Seller shall pay the
     Buyer a Volume True-Up Payment calculated as:

          True-Up Payment = (Minimum Annual Production - Actual Annual Volume)
                            x Strike Price x 0.35

     where Strike Price is as defined in Rule PM-1 (Article 8.1). This payment
     compensates the Buyer for the reduced hedging benefit when production
     underperforms expectations. Volumes excluded from Contracted Volume due
     to Force Majeure under Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5), grid curtailment under
     Rule ED-2 (Article 5.3), and negative pricing curtailment under Rule ED-3
     (Article 5.4) shall be added back to Actual Annual Volume for purposes
     of this calculation. The True-Up Payment is calculated and settled as
     part of the Annual Reconciliation under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.2).

     Linked Rules: VT-2, PM-1, EV-4, ED-2, ED-3, EV-2.

7.4  RULE PY-4 (Certificate Late Delivery Penalty): If the Seller fails to
     deliver GoOs by the Certificate Delivery Deadline specified in Rule CD-2
     (Article 6.2), the Seller shall pay a late delivery penalty of
     EUR 0.75 per MWh per calendar month of delay (or part thereof), applied
     to the volume of GoOs not yet delivered. The penalty accrues from the
     Certificate Delivery Deadline until the earlier of: (a) actual delivery
     of the GoOs, or (b) payment of the certificate shortfall amount under
     Rule CD-3 (Article 6.3). This penalty is separate from and cumulative
     with the certificate shortfall payment under Rule CD-3 and the CfD
     Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: CD-2, CD-3, PY-1, CD-1.

7.5  RULE PY-5 (Netting and Credit Support): Settlement Amounts due in the
     same direction for a given Settlement Period shall be netted. The Party
     with the larger aggregate net exposure (determined quarterly on a
     mark-to-market basis) shall post collateral in the form of a bank
     guarantee or standby letter of credit equal to the greater of (a) three
     (3) months' estimated CfD Settlement Amounts based on forward OMIE prices
     and projected generation under Rule VT-4 (Article 9.5), or (b)
     EUR 3,000,000. Collateral shall be issued by a financial institution with
     a minimum credit rating of BBB+ (S&P) or Baa1 (Moody's). Credit support
     requirements are reviewed annually as part of the Annual Reconciliation
     under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.2).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, VT-4, EV-2, EV-5.

7.6  RULE PY-6 (Tax Gross-Up): All payments under this Agreement shall be made
     free and clear of any withholding or deduction for taxes. If either Party
     is required by law to make any such deduction, the paying Party shall
     gross up the payment such that the receiving Party receives the full
     amount it would have received absent such deduction. Each Party shall
     use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize withholding taxes,
     including by claiming relief under the Spain-Germany double tax treaty.
     Tax adjustments affect the net Settlement Amount under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1) and are reconciled as part of the Annual Reconciliation
     under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.2).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, EV-2, PY-2.

ARTICLE 8: PRICE INDEXATION AND MARKET DATA
================================================================================

8.1  RULE PM-1 (Strike Price): The Strike Price shall be EUR 42.50 per MWh,
     fixed for the duration of the Term, subject only to (a) adjustments
     arising from a Regulatory Change Event under Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4),
     and (b) the CPI escalation mechanism in Rule PM-4 (Article 8.4). The
     Strike Price reflects the Seller's levelized cost of energy and the
     Buyer's assessment of long-term Iberian wholesale price expectations.
     The Strike Price is applied in the CfD Settlement Calculation under
     Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) and the Volume True-Up Payment under Rule PY-3
     (Article 7.4).

     Linked Rules: PY-1, PY-3, EV-3, PM-4.

8.2  RULE PM-2 (Reference Price - OMIE Day-Ahead): The Reference Price for
     each hour shall be the OMIE day-ahead marginal price for the Spanish
     zone, as published by OMIE on www.omie.es by 13:00 CET D-1 (following
     the daily market coupling session). The Reference Price constitutes the
     market benchmark against which the CfD settles. It is used directly
     in the Plant-Weighted Average Price calculation under Rule PM-3
     (Article 8.3) and indirectly in the CfD Settlement under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1). The Reference Price also determines the applicability
     of the negative pricing curtailment provision under Rule ED-3
     (Article 5.4).

     Linked Rules: PM-3, PY-1, ED-3, PM-4.

8.3  RULE PM-3 (Plant-Weighted Average Price): For each Settlement Period,
     the Plant-Weighted Average Price ("PWAP") shall be calculated as:

          PWAP = SUM(h=1 to H) [Reference Price(h) x Portfolio Output(h)]
                 / SUM(h=1 to H) [Portfolio Output(h)]

     where:
     - Reference Price(h) is the OMIE day-ahead price for hour (h) per
       Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2);
     - Portfolio Output(h) is the combined metered output of both Facilities
       for hour (h) per Rule VT-1 (Article 9.1);
     - H is the total number of hours in the Settlement Period.

     The PWAP captures the solar generation profile and represents the
     effective market price the Facilities achieve. The PWAP is used as the
     variable price component in the CfD Settlement under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1). This approach means the Buyer bears capture rate risk,
     as acknowledged in Rule OV-2 (Article 4.2), since the PWAP will
     typically be lower than the flat time-weighted average OMIE price due
     to the midday solar production concentration and merit-order
     cannibalization effect. The PWAP methodology also interacts with the
     negative pricing curtailment provision under Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4):
     hours in which the Seller curtails under Rule ED-3 are excluded from
     both the numerator and denominator of the PWAP calculation.

     Linked Rules: PM-2, VT-1, PY-1, OV-2, ED-3.

8.4  RULE PM-4 (CPI Escalation): The Strike Price shall be adjusted annually
     on 1 January of each year, commencing 1 January 2027, by seventy percent
     (70%) of the year-on-year change in the Spanish Consumer Price Index
     (Indice de Precios al Consumo, "IPC") as published by the Instituto
     Nacional de Estadistica (INE) for the twelve-month period ending
     30 September of the preceding year. The adjustment shall be cumulative
     and compounding. For the avoidance of doubt, the adjustment can result
     in both increases and decreases to the Strike Price. The escalated Strike
     Price shall be used in all settlement calculations under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1) from the effective date of adjustment. In the event that
     the IPC is not published or is discontinued, the Parties shall use the
     Eurostat Harmonized Index of Consumer Prices (HICP) for Spain as a
     substitute. The CPI-adjusted Strike Price also applies to the Volume
     True-Up calculation under Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4) and the negative
     pricing threshold assessment interacting with Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4)
     is NOT adjusted -- the Curtailment Threshold remains at a fixed
     -EUR 20.00/MWh.

     Linked Rules: PM-1, PY-1, PY-3, ED-3, PM-2.

8.5  RULE PM-5 (Market Data Fallback): In the event that OMIE fails to publish
     the day-ahead price for any hour(s), the Reference Price shall be
     determined in the following order of priority: (a) the OMIE intraday
     session 1 price for the same hour(s); (b) the Portuguese MIBEL zone
     day-ahead price for the same hour(s) as published by OMIE; (c) the
     average OMIE day-ahead price for the same hour(s) across the preceding
     seven (7) calendar days; (d) if none of the foregoing are available for
     more than seventy-two (72) consecutive hours, the Parties shall suspend
     CfD settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1) for the affected period
     and negotiate in good faith a substitute reference price within thirty
     (30) days. Fallback determinations shall be documented in the settlement
     statement under Rule PY-2 (Article 7.2).

     Linked Rules: PM-2, PY-1, PY-2, VT-1.

ARTICLE 9: VOLUME AND TIMESERIES
================================================================================

9.1  RULE VT-1 (Contracted Volume Measurement): The Contracted Volume for
     each hour shall be the aggregate net electrical energy (in MWh, to three
     decimal places) injected by Facility Alpha and Facility Beta at their
     respective Grid Connection Points, as recorded by the fiscal meters
     specified in Rule ED-5 (Article 5.5). The following adjustments apply:
     (a) transformer losses between inverter output and Grid Connection Point
     are already reflected in the fiscal meter readings; (b) volumes curtailed
     under Rule ED-3 (Article 5.4) during a Negative Price Period are excluded;
     (c) volumes curtailed due to grid instruction under Rule ED-2
     (Article 5.3) are excluded. The Contracted Volume forms the basis for
     the CfD Settlement under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1), the Plant-Weighted
     Average Price under Rule PM-3 (Article 8.3), and the GoO entitlement
     under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1).

     Linked Rules: ED-5, ED-3, ED-2, PY-1, PM-3, CD-1.

9.2  RULE VT-2 (Minimum Annual Production Guarantee): The Seller guarantees a
     Minimum Annual Production equal to eighty percent (80%) of the combined
     P50 yield estimate for both Facilities (the "Minimum Annual Production").
     Based on the P50 estimates in Schedule B:

          Facility Alpha (300 MWp): P50 = 525 GWh/yr -> Minimum = 420 GWh/yr
          Facility Beta (200 MWp):  P50 = 370 GWh/yr -> Minimum = 296 GWh/yr
          Portfolio Total:          P50 = 895 GWh/yr -> Minimum = 716 GWh/yr

     The Minimum Annual Production is assessed annually as part of the Annual
     Reconciliation under Rule EV-2 (Article 10.2). Failure to meet the
     Minimum Annual Production triggers the Volume True-Up Payment under
     Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4). Deemed production for Force Majeure periods
     under Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5), grid curtailment under Rule ED-2
     (Article 5.3), and negative pricing curtailment under Rule ED-3
     (Article 5.4) shall be added back for purposes of this assessment.

     Linked Rules: EV-2, PY-3, EV-4, ED-2, ED-3, VT-1, ED-4.

9.3  RULE VT-3 (Capture Rate Monitoring): The Seller shall calculate and report
     to the Buyer monthly the Capture Rate for each Facility and the portfolio,
     defined as:

          Capture Rate = PWAP (per Rule PM-3) / Time-Weighted Average OMIE
                         Day-Ahead Price

     where the Time-Weighted Average OMIE Day-Ahead Price is the simple
     arithmetic mean of all hourly OMIE day-ahead prices in the Settlement
     Period. Typical expected Capture Rates for Iberian solar are 75-90% in
     early years, potentially declining as solar penetration increases. If the
     trailing twelve-month Capture Rate falls below sixty-five percent (65%),
     the Buyer may request a market review to assess whether the capture rate
     decline is structural or temporary, and the Parties shall discuss in good
     faith any commercially reasonable mitigation measures. The Capture Rate
     interacts with the shape risk allocation acknowledged in Rule OV-2
     (Article 4.2) and affects the effective value of the CfD to the Buyer
     under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1).

     Linked Rules: PM-3, OV-2, PY-1, VT-1.

9.4  RULE VT-4 (Performance Ratio and Availability): The Seller shall maintain
     each Facility at a minimum technical availability of ninety-five percent
     (95%) on a rolling twelve-month basis, excluding scheduled maintenance
     under Rule ED-4 (Article 5.5) and Force Majeure under Rule EV-4
     (Article 10.5). The Performance Ratio (PR) for each Facility shall be
     calculated monthly as:

          PR = Actual Output / (GHI x Panel Area x Nominal Efficiency)

     using Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) data from on-site pyranometers
     and satellite-derived data (Solargis or equivalent). If the trailing
     twelve-month PR falls below seventy-five percent (75%) for either
     Facility, the Seller shall commission an Independent Technical Assessment
     at its cost. Availability and PR data feed into the production assessment
     under Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3) and the production forecast under
     Rule VT-5 (Article 9.6).

     Linked Rules: ED-4, EV-4, VT-2, VT-5, VT-1.

9.5  RULE VT-5 (Production Forecasting): The Seller shall provide the Buyer
     with: (a) a day-ahead generation forecast by 10:00 CET D-1, updated at
     18:00 CET D-1 following OMIE market results; (b) a monthly generation
     forecast by the 25th of the preceding month; and (c) an annual generation
     forecast by 15 November of the preceding year. Forecasts shall specify
     expected hourly output by Facility and portfolio aggregate. While
     forecasts do not modify contractual obligations, they enable the Buyer
     to manage its own hedging positions. Systematic forecast deviation
     exceeding 25% on a quarterly rolling basis shall trigger a consultation
     process. Forecast accuracy interacts with the Buyer's exposure management
     under Rule PY-5 (Article 7.6) and the production guarantees under
     Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3).

     Linked Rules: PY-5, VT-2, VT-4, PM-3.

10.2 RULE EV-2 (Annual Reconciliation): Within one hundred and twenty (120)
     days after the end of each calendar year, the Parties shall conduct an
     Annual Reconciliation comprising: (a) verification of aggregate
     Contracted Volume against the Minimum Annual Production under Rule VT-2
     (Article 9.3); (b) calculation and settlement of any Volume True-Up
     Payment under Rule PY-3 (Article 7.4); (c) reconciliation of GoO
     deliveries against the Contracted Volume under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1)
     and verification of timely delivery under Rule CD-2 (Article 6.2);
     (d) application of the CPI escalation to the Strike Price under

     of credit support adequacy under Rule PY-5 (Article 7.6); (f) assessment
     of Capture Rate trends under Rule VT-3 (Article 9.4); and (g) review of
     any pending Regulatory Change Events under Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4).
     The Annual Reconciliation statement shall be binding absent manifest
     error, subject to the dispute resolution procedures in Article 12.

     Linked Rules: VT-2, PY-3, CD-1, CD-2, PM-4, PY-5, VT-3, EV-3.

10.3 RULE EV-3 (Regulatory Change): A "Regulatory Change Event" means any
     change in Spanish or EU law, regulation, government decree, or binding
     regulatory decision occurring after the Effective Date that directly and
     materially affects: (a) the OMIE market design or price formation
     mechanism, impacting the Reference Price under Rule PM-2 (Article 8.2);
     (b) the GoO issuance, transfer, or recognition regime, impacting
     certificate delivery under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1); (c) taxation of
     renewable energy generation or PPAs (including but not limited to the
     Spanish 7% generation tax under Ley 15/2012 and any successor levies);
     (d) grid access or connection charges affecting the economics of the
     Facilities; (e) the introduction of mandatory curtailment regimes,
     capacity mechanisms, or renewable energy auctions that directly compete
     with or constrain the CfD.

     Upon the occurrence of a Regulatory Change Event, either Party may serve
     written notice within ninety (90) days requesting renegotiation of the
     affected terms, specifically the Strike Price under Rule PM-1
     (Article 8.1), the settlement methodology under Rule PY-1 (Article 7.1),
     or the certificate framework under Rule CD-1 (Article 6.1). If the
     Parties cannot reach agreement within one hundred and fifty (150) days,
     either Party may refer the matter to expert determination under
     Article 12 or, in cases of fundamental economic disruption, exercise
     termination rights under Rule EV-5 (Article 10.6).

     Linked Rules: PM-2, CD-1, PM-1, PY-1, EV-5, OV-3.

10.4 RULE EV-4 (Force Majeure): A "Force Majeure Event" means any event or
     circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party that
     prevents or materially impairs performance of its obligations, including
     but not limited to: (a) natural disasters (earthquakes, floods, wildfires,
     volcanic eruption); (b) grid failure or transmission infrastructure
     damage not caused by the Seller; (c) acts of war, terrorism, civil
     unrest, or sanctions; (d) pandemic or epidemic declared by WHO; (e)
     changes in law that make performance illegal (as distinct from merely
     more expensive, which is addressed under Rule EV-3 (Article 10.4)).

     The affected Party shall notify the other Party within five (5) business
     days of becoming aware of a Force Majeure Event, specifying the nature,
     expected duration, and affected volumes. During a Force Majeure Event,
     the affected Party's obligations under Rule ED-1 (Article 5.1) shall be
     suspended. Deemed production for Force Majeure hours shall be calculated
     using satellite irradiance data for the affected Facility's location and
     the trailing 12-month Performance Ratio under Rule VT-4 (Article 9.5),
     and shall be added back to Actual Annual Volume for the Minimum Annual
     Production assessment under Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3). If a Force Majeure
     Event continues for more than one hundred and eighty (180) consecutive
     days, either Party may terminate this Agreement under Rule EV-5
     (Article 10.6).

     Linked Rules: ED-1, EV-3, VT-4, VT-2, EV-5, PY-3.

10.5 RULE EV-5 (Termination): Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon
     written notice in the following circumstances: (a) material unremedied
     breach persisting for ninety (90) days after written notice (including
     persistent non-payment of CfD Settlement Amounts under Rule PY-1
     (Article 7.1) exceeding EUR 3,000,000); (b) insolvency, administration,
     or winding-up of the other Party; (c) a Force Majeure Event under
     Rule EV-4 (Article 10.5) continuing for more than one hundred and eighty
     (180) consecutive days; (d) a Regulatory Change Event under Rule EV-3
     (Article 10.4) that the Parties cannot resolve and that renders the
     Agreement fundamentally uneconomic; (e) aggregate Contracted Volume
     falling below sixty percent (60%) of the Minimum Annual Production under
     Rule VT-2 (Article 9.3) for two (2) consecutive years, after adding back
     excluded volumes.

     Upon termination, the terminating Party shall pay a Termination Amount
     equal to the net present value of the remaining expected CfD Settlement
     Amounts for the unexpired Term, calculated using forward OMIE prices
     from the most recent OMIP futures curve and discounted at the EUR
     mid-swap rate plus 1.50%. If the Termination Amount is positive for the
     non-defaulting Party, it shall be payable by the defaulting Party. The
     credit support under Rule PY-5 (Article 7.6) may be drawn upon to
     satisfy the Termination Amount.

     Linked Rules: PY-1, EV-4, EV-3, VT-2, PY-5.

10.6 RULE EV-6 (Grid Connection Milestones): The Seller shall maintain in
     full force and effect the Grid Connection Agreements with REE for both
     Facilities throughout the Term. If any Grid Connection Agreement is
     terminated, suspended, or materially amended by REE, the Seller shall
     notify the Buyer within five (5) business days. If a Grid Connection
     Agreement termination results in a Facility being unable to inject
     electricity for more than ninety (90) consecutive days, the affected
     Facility shall be excluded from the Contracted Volume under Rule VT-1
     (Article 9.1), the Minimum Annual Production under Rule VT-2
     (Article 9.3) shall be adjusted downward proportionally, and the Buyer
     may exercise its termination rights under Rule EV-5 (Article 10.6) if
     the remaining Contracted Capacity falls below 300 MW. Grid connection
     status also affects the GoO issuance eligibility under Rule CD-1
     (Article 6.1).

     Linked Rules: VT-1, VT-2, EV-5, CD-1, ED-2.

ARTICLE 12: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

12.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall
     first be submitted to senior management of both Parties for resolution
     within thirty (30) days.

12.2 If not resolved through senior management discussions, disputes relating
     to technical matters (including metering under Rule ED-5, production
     assessment under Rule VT-2, and Capture Rate under Rule VT-3) shall be
     referred to an independent technical expert appointed by mutual agreement
     or, failing agreement within fifteen (15) days, by the International
     Chamber of Commerce (ICC).

12.3 All other disputes shall be referred to arbitration under the Rules of the
     ICC International Court of Arbitration. The arbitral tribunal shall
     consist of three (3) arbitrators. The seat of arbitration shall be Paris,
     France. The language of arbitration shall be English. The arbitral award
     shall be final and binding.
