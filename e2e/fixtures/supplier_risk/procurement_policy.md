# Procurement Risk Policy PRP-7 (Supplier Risk Desk)

Policy marker: PRP7_SUPPLIER_POLICY_MARKER

## 1. Scope

This policy governs how the supplier risk desk classifies and acts on
supplier risk for every component that feeds the Lyon and Brno plants.

## 2. Risk tiers and required actions

- Red tier (risk score 60 or above): the category manager must open a
  dual-sourcing plan within 10 business days and escalate to the VP of
  Supply Chain. Purchase orders above EUR 250,000 require a second signature.
- Amber tier (35 to 59): quarterly review, a safety stock of at least
  six weeks for any single-sourced component.
- Green tier (below 35): annual review only.

## 3. Single-source components

A component with only one qualified supplier is a concentration risk. If the
supplier of a single-sourced component is red tier, the plant that consumes
that component must be named in the escalation and a temporary safety stock of
eight weeks is mandatory.

## 4. Country risk

Suppliers in high country-risk jurisdictions need a documented exit plan,
regardless of tier.

## 5. Approvals

Any decision to stop buying from a supplier needs human approval from the VP
of Supply Chain before it is communicated.
