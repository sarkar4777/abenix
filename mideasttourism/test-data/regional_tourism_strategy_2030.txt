THE GULF REGION — REGIONAL TOURISM STRATEGY 2030
=========================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
Tourism Strategy 2030 positions tourism as a key pillar of economic
diversification across the region, targeting 150 million annual visits by 2030
and a tourism sector contribution of 10% to GDP (up from 3.5% in 2023).

The strategy outlines five pillars:

1. MAJOR EVENTS & SPORTS TOURISM
   - Stadium and arena capacity for 30 million event visitors annually by 2030
   - Year-round calendar anchored on Formula 1, football and esports
   - Investment: USD 250 billion in event and transport infrastructure
   - Target: 50 million event-driven visitors annually

2. LEISURE & ENTERTAINMENT DESTINATIONS
   - Flagship developments: Lusail, Yas Island, Saadiyat, Palm Jebel Ali
   - Seasonal festivals: Dubai Shopping Festival, Abu Dhabi Season, Muscat Nights
   - Target: 70 million leisure visitors annually
   - Hotel capacity expansion: 500,000 new rooms by 2030

3. CULTURAL & HERITAGE TOURISM
   - UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Bahla Fort, Al Ain Cultural Sites,
     Bat archaeological complex, Qalhat, Land of Frankincense
   - New museum network: national museum expansion, regional museums
   - Investment: USD 50 billion in cultural infrastructure
   - Target: 15 million cultural tourists annually

4. BUSINESS & MICE TOURISM
   - Dubai as a global business hub (F1, boxing, esports, conferences)
   - New convention centres and expo facilities across the region
   - Target: 10 million business visitors annually
   - Investment: USD 80 billion in MICE infrastructure

5. ECO-TOURISM & ADVENTURE
   - Salalah monsoon-season park development
   - Coral reef preservation and diving tourism on the Gulf coast
   - Mountain tourism in the Hajar range and the Dhofar highlands
   - Desert safari experiences in the Empty Quarter
   - Target: 5 million eco-tourists annually

INVESTMENT PIPELINE
-------------------
Total committed investment: USD 320 billion

Major projects:
- Lusail waterfront city: USD 45 billion — mixed-use marina and stadium district
- Coastal Resorts Programme: USD 28 billion — luxury coastal resorts
- Saadiyat Cultural District: USD 12 billion — museums and wellness tourism
- Entertainment City: USD 30 billion — entertainment district near Dubai
- Heritage Quarter: USD 75 billion — restored old-town cultural destination
- Nizwa Development: USD 50 billion — heritage and desert tourism

REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES
-------------------------------
1. Dubai: Business hub, entertainment capital (Shopping Festival, Expo, F1)
2. Doha: Major-event host, museum quarter, corniche regeneration
3. Muscat: Cultural heritage, souks and forts, green city development
4. Abu Dhabi: Coastal leisure, island resorts, cultural district
5. Northern Emirates: Industrial tourism, cultural centres, island resorts
6. Lusail: Future city, marina district, stadium legacy venues
7. Nizwa: UNESCO heritage, desert resorts, cultural experiences
8. Salalah: Monsoon tourism, eco-tourism, summer escape destination
9. Sharjah: Archaeological sites, Gulf coast, adventure tourism

VISA & ACCESS STRATEGY
-----------------------
- E-visa expansion to 75+ countries (from 49 in 2023)
- Visa-on-arrival for GCC residents of any nationality
- Transit visa (96-hour stopover programme)
- Special event visas (F1, National Day, Eid, regional seasons)
- Target: reduce visa processing to under 5 minutes by 2026

SUSTAINABILITY COMMITMENT
--------------------------
- 50% renewable energy for tourism facilities by 2030
- Zero-waste resorts (Coastal Resorts Programme, Saadiyat)
- Coral reef restoration programme (100,000 coral fragments)
- Carbon-neutral tourism operations in Lusail
- Biodiversity preservation: Arabian leopard, marine reserves

This strategy document is issued by the Ministry of Tourism,
Middle East region, under the guidance of Tourism Strategy 2030.
